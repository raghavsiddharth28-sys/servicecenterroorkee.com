Common Pureit RO Problems We Handle in Roorkee

Below are 36 frequent faults our technicians diagnose and resolve on-site for Pureit purifiers:

#1

Pureit RO Not Turning On (Power Dead)

"Pureit RO bilkul on nahi ho raha, switch on karne par lights aur sound band hain."

Commonly caused by a burned-out SMPS power adapter, blown internal fuse, or a tripped Low Pressure Switch (LPS) when municipal or borewell tap pressure is insufficient.

#2

Pureit Low Water Flow (Trickle Flow)

"Dispenser se paani boond-boond ya bahut slow stream me nikal raha hai."

Usually indicates a clogged external spun pre-filter, saturated inline sediment filter, or calcium scaling across the Pureit RO membrane.

#3

Pureit Storage Tank Not Filling

"Machine ghanto chalne ke baad bhi storage tank poori tarah khali rehta hai."

A stuck solenoid valve (SV), closed auto-shutoff valve, or completely blocked membrane pushes all incoming feed water to drain rather than filling the clean tank.

#4

Pureit Water Leakage from Filter Housing or Base

"Pureit purifier ke niche se ya body ke joints se lagatar paani tapak raha hai."

Cracked filter bowl, dried rubber O-ring seal, or loose 1/4" push-fit elbow fittings cause internal water seepage.

#5

Pureit Booster Pump Making Loud Vibrating Noise

"Pump se bahut tez vibration ya khad-khad ki aawaz aa rahi hai."

Occurs when internal pump bearings wear out, air locks enter the pump head, or rubber vibration-isolation mounts loosen on the chassis.

#6

Pureit Reject Water Draining Continuously

"Tank full hone ke baad bhi drain pipe se paani lagatar beh raha hai."

Caused by a mechanically jammed Solenoid Valve (SV) or failing Auto Shut-Off Valve (ASV) that fails to seal when back-pressure builds.

#7

Pureit Bad, Bitter or Sour Taste in Water

"Paani ka taste kadwa, chalky ya ajeeb lag raha hai."

An exhausted post-carbon polishing filter or depleted mineralizer cartridge fails to neutralize acidity, spoiling the fresh taste.

#8

Pureit Foul Smell or Stale Odor from Storage Tank

"Paani me se ajeeb si badboo ya stale smell aa rahi hai."

Storage tank requires sanitization to remove bacterial biofilm, or activated carbon has reached chlorine-adsorption saturation.

#9

Pureit High TDS Output (TDS Not Reducing)

"Pure water ka TDS raw water ke barabar aa raha hai."

Indicates a ruptured RO membrane, improper membrane seating in its housing, or an excessively opened manual TDS bypass screw.

#10

Pureit Extremely Low TDS Output (< 30 PPM)

"TDS bahut hi zyada kam ho gaya hai jisse paani tasteless lag raha hai."

The membrane has stripped all natural minerals without proper re-mineralization or TDS blending, requiring recalibration of the needle valve.

#11

Pureit Warning Beep or Filter Indicator Light

"Front panel par filter alarm buzzer ya LED indicator blink kar raha hai."

Alerts homeowners that the scheduled operating hours or flow volume has been reached, requiring cartridge replacement and system reset.

#12

Pureit Solenoid Valve (SV) Jammed or Humming

"SV valve khul nahi rahi ya continuous humming sound kar rahi hai."

Rust, silt particles, or electrical coil burnout prevent the 24V DC solenoid plunger from actuating properly.

#13

Pureit Auto Cut-Off Switch Failure (Tank Overflow)

"Tank bharne ke baad bhi machine band nahi hoti aur paani girne lagta hai."

The magnetic float switch or microswitch lever inside the storage reservoir is jammed with scale or has failed electrically.

#14

Pureit External Pre-Filter Candle Choking Rapidly

"Bahar laga white spun candle 1-2 mahine me hi dark brown/kala ho jata hai."

Heavy seasonal sediment and rust in Roorkee tap water choke 5-micron spun fibers, starving the booster pump of feed volume.

#15

Pureit Pre-Filter Housing Threads Leaking

"Spun candle change karne ke baad housing ke threads se paani leak ho raha hai."

Misaligned, pinched, or un-lubricated rubber O-ring seal between the housing bowl and mounting cap allows pressure leakage.

#16

Pureit Booster Pump Head Diaphragm Leakage

"Pump ke agle hisse (head) se paani tapak raha hai."

High operating line pressure ruptures the flexible rubber diaphragm inside the pump head, which can be replaced without replacing the entire motor.

#17

Pureit SMPS Power Adapter Heating Excessively

"Power adapter bahut zyada garm ho raha hai aur jalne ki smell aa rahi hai."

Pump motor aging causes excessive electrical current draw (>1.8A), overheating secondary coils inside the SMPS adapter.

#18

Pureit SMPS Voltage Drop Under Motor Load

"Machine start hote hi voltage drop ho jata hai aur pump jhatke leta hai."

A degraded capacitor in the SMPS supply shows 24V DC at idle but collapses to 12V DC under load, stalling the pump.

#19

Pureit Complete RO Membrane Scaling Blockage

"Hard water se membrane poori tarah jam ho chuki hai."

Calcium and magnesium carbonates form mineral scale over the 0.0001-micron pores, choking clean water permeate production.

#20

Pureit Zero Reject Water Coming Out of Drain

"Wastage pipe se paani nikalna bilkul band ho gaya hai."

A blocked Flow Restrictor (FR) stops drain flow, which will ruin the RO membrane within hours due to mineral crystallization.

#21

Pureit Excessive Reject Water Output (10:1 Ratio)

"Drain pipe se bohot zyada paani beh raha hai par pure water zero hai."

Flow Restrictor has lost internal flow resistance or the membrane is choked, routing all incoming water straight to drain.

#22

Pureit Dispensing Tap / Faucet Dripping Constantly

"Kitchen tap se boond-boond paani sink ya counter par tapak raha hai."

Internal silicone washer or ceramic disc inside the dispensing faucet has worn down from daily repetitive operations.

#23

Pureit Cloudy or Milky Water in First Dispense

"Glass me nikalne par paani milky ya white dikhta hai."

Harmless micro-air bubbles trapped in fresh inline filter media that clear within 60 seconds; persistent cloudiness requires filter bypass check.

#24

Pureit Low Pressure Switch (LPS) False Tripping

"Inlet paani aane par bhi LPS machine ko start nahi hone de raha."

Sediment in the 1/4" feed line or a weak spring diaphragm inside the LPS prevents contact closure at standard operating pressures.

#25

Pureit High Pressure Switch (HPS) Failure

"Pressurized tank me back-pressure banne par bhi machine shut off nahi hoti."

Microswitch contacts welded closed or diaphragm rupture prevents the HPS from cutting circuit power.

#26

Pureit Mineral / Alkaline Cartridge Depletion

"Purifier ka mineral addition stage expire ho chuka hai."

Mineral balls dissolve over 10-12 months of daily use; an exhausted cartridge leaves water flat, acidic, and tasteless.

#27

Pureit UV Lamp Failure Alarm Beeping

"UV chamber ki light band hone se continuous alarm baj raha hai."

An 11W or 8W germicidal UV tube has fused, or the high-frequency electronic ballast has failed to ignite the lamp.

#28

Pureit UV Quartz Glass Sleeve Scaling

"UV chamber ke andar quartz glass par calcium layer jam gayi hai."

Hard mineral deposits cloud the quartz glass sleeve, blocking UV-C rays from reaching water pathogens.

#29

Pureit Ultrafiltration (UF) Capillary Choking

"UF hollow fiber cartridge jam hone se flow ruk gaya hai."

Colloidal silt blocks the 0.01-micron hollow fibers, requiring thorough reverse flushing or cartridge replacement.

#30

Pureit Internal Food-Grade Tubing Kinked

"Service ke dauran cabinet ke andar pipe mud gaya hai."

Sharp bends in stiff 1/4" PE tubing restrict feed water flow between filter stages inside compact chassis designs.

#31

Pureit Push-Fit Collar Lock Failure

"Pipe joint se achanak pipe bahar nikal gaya aur flooding ho gayi."

Worn stainless steel grab teeth inside quick-connect fittings slip under high pump surge pressures.

#32

Pureit Electronic PCB Controller Board Malfunction

"Machine ka circuit board fluctuate ho raha hai ya buttons freeze hain."

Voltage spikes or moisture ingress short-circuits PCB relays, causing erratic valve timing and false alarms.

#33

Pureit Auto-Flush Solenoid Remaining Open

"Auto-flush valve open hi reh gaya jisse system me pressure nahi ban raha."

Flush solenoid plunger stuck with calcium scale never closes, dumping pressurized water directly to drain.

#34

Pureit Storage Tank Air Valve / Bladder Deflation

"Under-sink tank me air pressure khatam ho gaya hai aur paani nahi nikal raha."

The butyl bladder loses its 6-8 PSI air pre-charge, causing the tank to feel heavy with water but unable to push water to the tap.

#35

Pureit Electrical Wiring Loose or Corroded

"Cabinet ke andar wire joints par carbon aa gaya hai."

High humidity inside purifier cabinets corrodes crimp terminals, leading to intermittent motor shutdowns.

#36

Pureit Commercial Plant Operating Pressure Drop

"Commercial plant me pressure gauge par reading 150 PSI se kam aa rahi hai."

Worn high-pressure pump impellers, choked pre-treatment media, or bypassed multiport valves drop operating pressure.

Pureit Problem → Cause → Solution Guide

Practical diagnostic matrix for common Pureit purifier breakdowns:

Problem Possible Cause Typical Service Solution
Purifier Completely Dead Burnt SMPS transformer, blown fuse, or tripped Low Pressure Switch (LPS). Test 24V DC output; inspect wall socket voltage; replace failed power adapter or LPS sensor.
Slow Flow / Trickle Stream Choked 5-micron pre-filter, scaled RO membrane, or pump pressure < 60 PSI. Replace outer spun candle; descaling or membrane replacement; booster pump head servicing.
Continuous Reject Drainage Solenoid Valve (SV) stuck open, failed Auto Shut-Off Valve (ASV), or float switch failure. Inspect 24V DC solenoid plunger; clean mineral scale or replace jammed SV; test float relay.
Filter Warning Alert / Buzzer Filter life counter reached, UV lamp failure, or electrical sensor fault. Install fresh compatible filter set; verify UV lamp continuity; perform board reset sequence.
Bad or Bitter Water Taste Exhausted post-carbon filter, acidic water due to very low TDS, or stagnant tank. Sanitize clean water storage tank; replace post-carbon cartridge; calibrate mineral balance.
