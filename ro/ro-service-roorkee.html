Purification Technologies Explained Simply

RO (Reverse Osmosis)

Uses a semi-permeable membrane sheet under pump pressure to separate dissolved heavy salts and minerals from drinking water.

UV (Ultra Violet)

Passes water through a stainless steel chamber with an ultraviolet lamp to neutralize waterborne bacteria and microbes.

UF (Ultra Filtration)

A hollow-fiber physical filter that removes fine suspended mud, rust particles, and cysts without requiring electrical pressure.

Common RO Purifier Problems Handled

Slow Water Flow / Tank Not Filling: Choked pre-carbon, spun candle, or blocked RO membrane.
Unusual or Bitter Taste: Exhausted post-carbon filter or mineral cartridge needing renewal.
Continuous Reject Water Draining: Malfunctioning auto-cut-off solenoid valve (SV) wasting water.
RO Machine Not Turning On: Burnt SMPS power supply adapter or faulty float switch.
Water Leaking from Pipe Elbows: Loose push-fit collet fittings or cracked filter housing bowl.

RO Service FAQs

The external spun pre-filter should typically be replaced every 3 to 6 months. Carbon and sediment cartridges are usually changed every 9 to 12 months, while the RO membrane typically lasts 18 to 24 months depending on groundwater TDS.
A continuously running RO usually means the auto-shutoff valve is stuck, or the booster pump cannot build enough backpressure because the membrane is choked or the storage tank air bladder is depleted.
