Common Fridge Problems We Handle in Roorkee

Refrigerator mein problem aane ke kai alag-alag electrical, mechanical ya refrigeration cycle reasons ho sakte hain. Har fault ka diagnosis appliance ke model aur symptoms par depend karta hai:

• Fridge Not Cooling / Weak Cooling

Compressor not running, low refrigerant charge, dirty condenser coils, or blocked air duct passage.

• Freezer Working but Lower Fridge Warm

Defrost heater failure, bi-metal sensor open, or frozen evaporator coil blocking airflow downward.

• Compressor Clicking & Shutting Off

Faulty PTC starter relay, open overload protector (OLP), or seized compressor internal piston.

• Water Leaking on Floor / Crisper

Choked defrost drain hole, cracked condensation drain pan, or disconnected drain tube.

• Excessive Ice Buildup / Freezing Everything

Damaged door gasket letting warm moist air inside, or thermostat stuck in closed contact.

• Unusual Humming or Rattling Noise

Worn evaporator fan motor bushing, condenser fan blade hitting debris, or loose mounting grommets.

Fault: Fridge running but cooling almost zero

Diagnostic Solution: Relay resistance measurement with digital multimeter and replacement with genuine rated relay kit.

Frost Free Refrigerator Repair in Roorkee

Frost free refrigerator repair service in Roorkee. Frost-free fridges eliminate the need for manual defrosting through an automated dynamic heating and fan-assisted cooling system.

1. Defrost System Architecture

Frost-free fridges contain a finned cooling coil concealed behind the freezer wall. Automated defrost system mein defrost timer/electronic controller, radiant defrost heater, bi-metal thermostat (cuts heater off at +10°C), and thermal safety fuse (blows at +72°C if heater overheats) ek synchronized sequence mein operate karte hain.

2. Typical Frost Free Diagnostics

Agar defrost sequence ka koi bhi ek component fail ho jaye toh cooling coil solid ice block ban jaati hai. Technician multimeter se heater resistance (typically 200–400 ohms) aur thermal fuse continuity check karke exact faulty component replace karte hain.

Inverter Refrigerator Repair in Roorkee

Inverter refrigerator repair near me in Roorkee. Inverter refrigerators conventional fixed-speed compressor ki jagah variable-speed BLDC (Brushless DC) compressor use karte hain jo internal cooling load ke according speed adjust karta hai.

1. Inverter Compressor vs Conventional Compressor

Standard fridge mein compressor 220V AC direct supply par on/off hota hai. Inverter fridge mein power board 220V AC ko DC voltage mein convert karta hai aur 3-phase variable frequency output se compressor ko 1100 RPM se 3600 RPM ke beech run karta hai. Isme traditional relay-OLP nahi hota; balki inverter controller board direct compressor windings ko drive karta hai.

2. Diagnostics & Inverter PCB Repair

Fault: Compressor silent, diagnostic LED on PCB blinks in pulse codes

Root Cause: IPM (Intelligent Power Module) IGBT breakdown on inverter board due to power surge, or compressor winding phase imbalance.

Diagnostic Solution: Winding resistance measurement across U, V, W terminals (must be identical, typically 8–15 ohms), IPM output wave check, and inverter PCB component-level repair.

Indicative Inverter Component Check: Inverter PCB Repair (₹1,500 - ₹2,800) | Inverter BLDC Testing (₹450 - ₹750).
