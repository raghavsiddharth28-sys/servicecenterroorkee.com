Common Microwave Faults Diagnosed

Microwave Not Heating: High voltage transformer, capacitor, diode, or magnetron vacuum tube failure.
Sparking & Burning Smell: Burnt mica cover sheet or metallic food splatter catching fire in microwave frequency path.
Glass Plate Not Turning: Faulty lower turntable synchron motor or broken drive coupler beneath plate.
Microwave Not Starting: Door microswitch contact failure preventing safety circuit from closing.
Display or Keypad Dead: Worn membrane keypad trace or power supply surge affecting logic board.
