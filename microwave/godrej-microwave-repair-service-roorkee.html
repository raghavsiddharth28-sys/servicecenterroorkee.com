Crucial Microwave Safety Warning — No DIY Opening

Warning: Lethal Electrical Voltage Stored Inside: Microwave ovens operate with an internal high-voltage step-up transformer and capacitor producing between 2,000 Volts and 2,500 Volts DC. Even when the microwave has been unplugged from the wall socket for days, the high-voltage capacitor can retain a lethal electrical charge if its internal bleeder resistor is open.

  • Never unscrew the outer metal shroud or attempt DIY testing on the magnetron, capacitor, or transformer.
  • Never run a microwave oven with a cracked glass door, warped door hinges, or damaged door mesh.
  • If you observe continuous sparking, smoke, or a burning plastic odor, immediately switch off the wall socket and arrange professional inspection.
