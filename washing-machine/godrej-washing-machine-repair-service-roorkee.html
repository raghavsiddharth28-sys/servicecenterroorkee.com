Common Godrej Washing Machine Problems We Check

Common Godrej washing machine issues our technicians diagnose in Roorkee homes:

Water Not Filling in Automatic Machine (E1 Error): The water inlet valve mesh filter is choked with sediment, or the inlet solenoid coil is open-circuit.
Drain Water Timeout (E2 Error Code): The drain hose is kinked, or the rubber drain valve is blocked by coins, safety pins, or fabric threads.
Violent Tub Banging During Spin (E3 Error): The 4-corner tub suspension damping rods have lost grease, or the laundry is clumped to one side.
Semi-Automatic Spin Basket Not Running: The top lid safety switch contacts are burnt, or the spin motor run capacitor rating has dropped.
Pulsator Plate Does Not Turn but Motor Hums: The motor drive belt has slipped off the pulley, or the pulsator center splines are stripped.
Water Continuously Draining Onto Floor: The drain valve rubber seal has debris caught beneath it, preventing a complete watertight seal.

Godrej Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Godrej digital panels show clear error codes when a wash cycle is interrupted:

E1 Water fill failure.
Possible Reason: Water has not reached the programmed level within the allowed time limit.
What You Can Check: Check that the water tap is open and clean the mesh filter at the back of the inlet hose.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician tests the water inlet solenoid valve and pressure switch.
E2 Drain error.
Possible Reason: Dirty water has not been discharged from the tub within the allowed time.
What You Can Check: Ensure the drain hose is not bent or kinked, and inspect the coin trap.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the drain valve rubber, linkage, and drain motor.
E3 Unbalanced load.
Possible Reason: Clothes have clumped to one side during spin acceleration.
What You Can Check: Pause the machine, spread garments evenly around the drum, and restart spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty drum continues to vibrate heavily, the 4-corner suspension damping rods need replacement.
E4 Top lid open warning.
Possible Reason: The safety control detects that the top lid is open during a spin cycle.
What You Can Check: Close the top lid firmly.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician tests the lid safety microswitch and wiring.
F1 / F2 Water level sensor circuit error.
Possible Reason: The pressure sensor reading is out of range or its air tube has disconnected.
What You Can Check: Ensure the machine is resting on flat, level ground.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the pressure sensor tube and transducer.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
