Common IFB Washing Machine Problems We Check

Common IFB washing machine issues our technicians diagnose in Roorkee homes:

Door Not Opening After Wash (Door Lock Delay): The bimetallic thermal door latch fails to release after cycle completion, or the internal door handle catch has cracked.
Water Not Draining Out (drn / dEr Code): The bottom drain coin filter is blocked with coins, safety pins, or fabric lint, or the drain pump motor has jammed.
Low Water Fill or Inlet Timeout (tAP / InLt Code): The Aqua Energie filter cartridge is choked, the inlet hose mesh has accumulated silt, or the solenoid valve coil has weakened.
Spin Cycle Unbalance Vibration (Unb Code): Worn out front load suspension shock absorbers or stripped splines on top load triadic pulsators.
Grinding Roaring Noise During Spin: The rear drum spider arm has developed cracks or water has entered and rusted the main tub ball bearings.
Water Not Heating (HEt Error): Hard water scale deposits have caused the heating element to burn out, or the temperature NTC sensor is faulty.

IFB Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

IFB digital control panels display short error codes to help identify what went wrong during a cycle:

drn / dEr Drain error.
Possible Reason: The washing machine cannot discharge dirty water within the prescribed program time.
What You Can Check: Check that the drain pipe is not bent or kinked, open the bottom coin trap, and clean out foreign objects.
Possible Service Requirement: If the filter is clean, the technician tests the electric drain pump motor, impeller, and drain wiring.
tAP / InLt Water tap or supply issue.
Possible Reason: Water is entering too slowly or not at all to satisfy the cycle requirement.
What You Can Check: Verify that the water tap is turned fully on and clean the mesh filter at the back of the inlet hose.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the Aqua Energie filter cartridge, dual solenoid inlet valve, or water level pressure switch.
dEL / dOr Door switch or latch error.
Possible Reason: The control board does not detect that the door latch is properly engaged before starting.
What You Can Check: Check that clothes are not caught in the door gasket, push the door shut firmly, and restart.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician tests the thermal door interlock switch, door handle mechanism, or PCB latch circuit.
Unb Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Laundry has gathered on one side of the drum, causing balance vibration during spin acceleration.
What You Can Check: Pause the cycle, open the door, redistribute clothes evenly around the drum, and resume spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty drum also triggers unbalance, the suspension shock struts or drum spider arm need inspection.
Pr5 Pressure sensor error.
Possible Reason: The electronic water level sensor or its air tube is disconnected, blocked, or malfunctioning.
What You Can Check: Ensure the machine is resting on a level floor.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician clears the pressure air chamber or replaces the electronic pressure transducer.
OE / OF Water overflow error.
Possible Reason: Water has exceeded the maximum safety level inside the tub.
What You Can Check: Turn off the water supply tap immediately.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the inlet solenoid valve for mechanical sticking and tests the water level switch.
HEt Heating element error.
Possible Reason: The machine is unable to heat water to the target temperature for hot wash cycles.
What You Can Check: Verify that the selected wash cycle uses hot water.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician tests the heating element resistance, NTC temperature thermistor, and PCB heater relay.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
