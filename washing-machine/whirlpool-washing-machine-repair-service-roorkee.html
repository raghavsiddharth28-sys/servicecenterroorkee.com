Top Load Pulsator Turns but Drum Does Not Spin: The center drive splines are stripped, or the transmission gearbox clutch spring is slipping under laundry load.

Whirlpool Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Whirlpool digital automatic panels display error codes when a cycle is interrupted. Common verified codes include:

F02 / E02 Drain timeout error.
Possible Reason: The machine cannot discharge dirty water within the allowed cycle time.
What You Can Check: Check that the drain pipe is not bent or blocked, and inspect the coin trap filter.
Possible Service Requirement: If the hose is clear, the electric drain pump motor or drain valve solenoid needs testing.
F01 / E01 Water inlet timeout.
Possible Reason: Water is entering too slowly or not at all to reach the required level.
What You Can Check: Check that the water tap is turned fully on and clean the sediment mesh filter at the back.
Possible Service Requirement: If tap flow is normal, the dual inlet solenoid valve or water level sensor requires testing.
F03 / E03 Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Laundry has shifted to one side of the tub during spin cycle acceleration.
What You Can Check: Open the top lid, redistribute clothes evenly across the drum, and restart spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty drum continues to trigger the code, the corner suspension rods need replacement.
F08 / dL Door or lid lock failure.
Possible Reason: The machine is unable to lock the lid or front door before starting high-speed spinning.
What You Can Check: Ensure the lid or door is closed firmly without laundry blocking the latch.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician must test the electric lid lock switch or front thermal door interlock.
F21 Drain pump electrical circuit fault.
Possible Reason: The electronic control board detects improper electrical resistance from the drain pump.
What You Can Check: Check if small objects like coins or threads are jammed inside the pump impeller.
Possible Service Requirement: The drain pump motor winding must be tested with a multimeter and replaced if open-circuit.
F06 Motor tachometer speed error.
Possible Reason: The motor cannot reach or maintain the correct spin speed commanded by the program.
What You Can Check: Make sure the machine is not overloaded with too many heavy water-soaked garments.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the drive belt tension, motor brushes, or motor speed hall sensor.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
