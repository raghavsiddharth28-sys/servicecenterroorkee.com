Common Lloyd Washing Machine Problems We Check

Common Lloyd washing machine problems our technicians diagnose in Roorkee:

Water Supply Timeout (E1 Error): Water enters too slowly or does not fill. Commonly caused by a choked inlet mesh filter, low tap pressure, or a faulty solenoid valve.
Water Drainage Timeout (E2 Error): The machine cannot pump out dirty water within the allowed time due to a blocked coin filter or faulty drain pump.
Door or Lid Open Error (E3 Error): The safety control detects that the door or top lid is not closed properly before spinning.
Spin Cycle Unbalance (E4 Error): The tub shakes heavily and stops spinning due to clumped clothes or worn suspension damping rods.
Water Level Sensor Fault (E5 Error): The electronic pressure switch fails to measure tub water level accurately, preventing the cycle from advancing.
Motor Driver Fault (E6 Error): The inverter motor driver detects abnormal rotation resistance or electrical overload.

Lloyd Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Lloyd digital washing machines display concise error codes to help locate the source of the problem:

E1 Water intake timeout.
Possible Reason: Water is not entering the tub at the required rate within the set time limit.
What You Can Check: Check that the water tap is turned fully on and clean the mesh filter inside the inlet hose connector.
Possible Service Requirement: If tap pressure is normal, the inlet solenoid valve or water level sensor requires technician testing.
E2 Drainage timeout.
Possible Reason: The washing machine cannot discharge dirty water within the programmed time.
What You Can Check: Check that the drain pipe is not bent or kinked, and clean out foreign objects from the drain filter.
Possible Service Requirement: If the drain line is clear, the technician inspects the electric drain pump motor or drain wiring.
E3 Door or lid open error.
Possible Reason: The safety control detects that the door or top lid is not closed properly before spinning.
What You Can Check: Close the door or lid firmly and verify that no clothes are obstructing the latch.
Possible Service Requirement: If the lid is closed but the code persists, the safety lid switch or electronic door lock mechanism needs testing.
E4 Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Clothes have clumped to one side of the tub, causing heavy shaking during spin acceleration.
What You Can Check: Pause the cycle, open the tub, distribute heavy garments evenly, and restart the spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty tub also shakes violently, the corner suspension dampening rods or spin balance ring need replacement.
E5 Water level sensor error.
Possible Reason: The electronic water level sensor or pressure tube is disconnected, blocked, or malfunctioning.
What You Can Check: Ensure the machine is resting on a level floor surface.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician clears the pressure air chamber or replaces the electronic pressure transducer.
E6 Motor driver fault.
Possible Reason: The control board detects abnormal motor rotation or motor driver overload.
What You Can Check: Turn off power for 5 minutes and check that the drum rotates freely by hand when empty.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician tests the motor winding resistance, motor capacitor, or inverter driver module.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
