Common Samsung Washing Machine Problems We Check

Our visiting technicians frequently diagnose and resolve these common Samsung washing machine issues in Roorkee:

Water Not Draining (5C / 5E Error): The machine cannot pump out dirty water. Usually caused by a blocked coin trap filter, bent drain hose, or a failed drain pump motor.
Water Supply Timeout (4C / 4E Error): Water does not fill the tub within the allowed time. Common causes include a clogged inlet mesh filter, low tap pressure, or a faulty solenoid inlet valve.
Spin Cycle Unbalance (UB / UE Error): The drum shakes violently and stops before reaching full spin speed. Caused by clumped laundry or worn corner suspension dampening rods.
Door Latch Delay or dC Error: The front load door fails to lock securely at cycle start, or the handle refuses to release after the wash finishes.
Loud Roaring Noise During Spin: A heavy jet-engine sound occurs during high-speed spinning, indicating that water has entered the rear tub ball bearings.
Display Panel Dead or Tripping Power: The machine shows no lights even when plugged into a working socket, caused by a blown fuse, noise filter fault, or main inverter PCB damage.

Samsung Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Samsung digital washing machines show short alphanumeric error codes on their screen. These codes help identify which component needs checking:

4C / 4E Water supply error.
Possible Reason: Water is not entering the tub at the required rate within the programmed time limit.
What You Can Check: Check that the water tap is turned on completely and verify the inlet hose mesh filter is clean and unbent.
Possible Service Requirement: If water pressure is normal but water does not enter, the inlet solenoid valve or pressure sensor needs technician testing.
5C / 5E Water drainage problem.
Possible Reason: The machine is unable to pump dirty water out of the drum before the spin cycle begins.
What You Can Check: Unscrew the coin filter at the bottom right of the front panel and remove any coins, hairpins, or lint.
Possible Service Requirement: If the filter is clean but water still does not drain, the electric drain pump motor or drain wiring requires inspection.
dC / dE Door or lid open error.
Possible Reason: The safety control detects that the door or lid is not securely closed and locked.
What You Can Check: Ensure no clothes are trapped between the door glass and rubber gasket, and close the door firmly.
Possible Service Requirement: If the door is closed properly but the code persists, the thermal door interlock switch or latch mechanism needs testing.
UB / UE Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Laundry has clumped to one side of the tub, causing dangerous balance vibration during spinning.
What You Can Check: Pause the cycle, open the tub, spread heavy items like towels or bedsheets evenly, and restart spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty tub also shakes violently, the 4-corner suspension dampening rods or spin balance ring need replacement.
LC / LE Water leakage detected.
Possible Reason: The safety float switch in the bottom base tray has detected water accumulation.
What You Can Check: Turn off the water tap and check around the machine base for active water puddles.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician must inspect internal hose connections, the door boot gasket seal, or tub joints for pinhole leaks.
3C / 3E Motor drive or tachometer fault.
Possible Reason: The electronic control board cannot detect proper motor rotation or the motor is overloaded.
What You Can Check: Reduce the wash load if the tub was packed with heavy blankets, and turn off power for 5 minutes.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician tests motor coil resistance, the hall tachometer sensor, or the inverter control module.
HE / HC Water heating error.
Possible Reason: The in-built heating element is not heating water to the selected temperature.
What You Can Check: Verify that the selected wash cycle requires warm or hot water.
Possible Service Requirement: The heating rod, NTC temperature sensor, or PCB heater relay needs electrical multimeter testing.
1C / 1E Water level sensor fault.
Possible Reason: The electronic pressure switch cannot accurately detect water depth inside the tub.
What You Can Check: Ensure the washing machine is sitting flat on level ground.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects the clear pressure hose for lint blockages or replaces the electronic pressure transducer.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
