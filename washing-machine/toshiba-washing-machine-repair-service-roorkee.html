Common Toshiba Washing Machine Problems We Check

Common Toshiba washing machine problems our technicians diagnose in Roorkee:

Water Drainage Failure (E1 Error): The machine cannot pump out rinse water. Caused by coins or lint caught in the drain filter, a bent drain hose, or a failed drain pump.
Door or Lid Open Error (E2 Error): The safety sensor detects that the door or lid is not securely shut during cycle start or spin mode.
Spin Imbalance (E3 Error): The drum knocks loudly against the casing and halts spin due to clumped clothes or worn suspension damping rods.
Water Fill Timeout (E4 Error): Water takes too long to fill the tub. Commonly caused by a clogged inlet mesh filter, low water pressure, or a faulty solenoid valve.
Water Level Sensor Fault (E5 Error): The electronic pressure switch fails to measure tub water level accurately, preventing the cycle from advancing.
Motor Rotation Fault (E7 Error): The S-DD inverter motor does not turn properly or the rotation hall sensor detects abnormal resistance.

Toshiba Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Toshiba digital washing machines display concise error codes to help locate the source of the problem:

E1 Drainage failure.
Possible Reason: The washing machine cannot drain dirty water out within the set cycle time.
What You Can Check: Check that the drain pipe is not kinked, check that the hose end is not submerged, and clean out the drain filter.
Possible Service Requirement: If the drain line is clear, the technician inspects the electric drain motor, impeller, and drain wiring.
E2 Door or lid open error.
Possible Reason: The safety control detects that the door or top lid is not closed properly before spinning.
What You Can Check: Close the door or lid firmly and verify that no clothes are obstructing the latch.
Possible Service Requirement: If the lid is closed but the code persists, the safety lid switch or electronic door lock mechanism needs testing.
E3 Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Clothes have clumped to one side of the tub, causing heavy shaking during spin acceleration.
What You Can Check: Pause the cycle, open the tub, distribute heavy garments evenly, and restart the spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty tub also shakes violently, the corner suspension dampening rods or spin balance ring need replacement.
E4 Water intake timeout.
Possible Reason: Water is entering too slowly or not at all within the programmed time limit.
What You Can Check: Ensure the water tap is turned fully on and clean the mesh filter inside the inlet hose connector.
Possible Service Requirement: If tap pressure is normal, the inlet solenoid valve or electronic pressure sensor requires technician testing.
E5 Water level sensor error.
Possible Reason: The electronic water level sensor or pressure tube is disconnected, blocked, or malfunctioning.
What You Can Check: Ensure the machine is resting on a level floor surface.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician clears the pressure air chamber or replaces the electronic pressure transducer.
E7 Motor drive or hall sensor fault.
Possible Reason: The control board does not receive proper rotation signals from the S-DD inverter motor.
What You Can Check: Turn off power for 5 minutes and check that the drum rotates freely by hand when empty.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician tests the motor hall tachometer sensor, motor drive inverter module, or motor windings.
E9 Water leakage fault.
Possible Reason: The internal sensor detects abnormal water loss or moisture in the bottom casing.
What You Can Check: Turn off the water supply tap and inspect the floor around the machine for standing water.
Possible Service Requirement: The technician inspects internal hose connections, tub joints, or drain seals for leaks.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
