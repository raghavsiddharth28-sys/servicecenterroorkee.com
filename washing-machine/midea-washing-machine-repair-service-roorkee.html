Common Midea Washing Machine Problems We Check

Our technicians commonly diagnose and resolve these Midea washing machine problems in Roorkee:

Water Fill Timeout (E10 Error): Water enters very slowly or does not fill. Commonly caused by a clogged inlet mesh filter, low water pressure, or a faulty solenoid valve.
Water Not Draining Out (E21 Error): Dirty water stays inside the drum. Usually caused by coins or lint blocking the drain filter, a bent hose, or a jammed drain pump motor.
Door Not Locking (E30 Error): The front loader door does not lock securely at the start of the wash, preventing the machine from spinning.
Unbalanced Load Vibration (E34 / E35 Error): The washer shakes heavily and halts during the spin cycle because clothes are bunched to one side or the suspension is worn.
Water Overflow (E12 Error): The machine continues to fill past the normal level due to an inlet valve that will not close or a blocked pressure sensor tube.
Lunar Dial Knob Not Responding: The program selection dial skips settings or freezes due to moisture buildup or an issue with the control circuit board.

Midea Washing Machine Error Codes & Diagnosis

Midea washing machines show short error codes on the digital screen to indicate what needs inspection:

E10 Water intake timeout.
Possible Reason: Water is not entering the machine at the required speed within the set time.
What You Can Check: Check that the water tap is turned fully on and clean the mesh filter inside the inlet hose connection.
Possible Service Requirement: If the water pressure is good but the error stays, the inlet solenoid valve or water level sensor needs testing.
E21 Drainage timeout.
Possible Reason: The washing machine cannot pump out dirty water within the allowed time limit.
What You Can Check: Open the small drain filter cap at the bottom front and clear out coins, threads, and hairpins.
Possible Service Requirement: If the drain filter and pipe are clear, the electric drain pump motor or internal wiring needs technician inspection.
E30 Door lock error.
Possible Reason: The safety control cannot confirm that the door is completely closed and locked.
What You Can Check: Check that no clothing is stuck in the door gasket, close the door firmly, and restart the cycle.
Possible Service Requirement: If the door is shut but will not lock, the electronic door interlock switch or door latch catch needs replacement.
E34 / E35 Unbalanced load fault.
Possible Reason: Laundry has gathered on one side of the tub, causing heavy shaking during high-speed spinning.
What You Can Check: Pause the cycle, open the tub, distribute heavy clothes evenly, and resume the spin.
Possible Service Requirement: If the empty drum also vibrates strongly, the tub suspension shock struts or balance sensors require replacement.
E12 Water overflow error.
Possible Reason: Water has risen higher than the maximum safety mark inside the tub.
What You Can Check: Turn off the water tap immediately to prevent water from spilling out.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician must test whether the inlet valve is stuck open mechanically or if the electronic pressure switch is faulty.
E64 Water heating error.
Possible Reason: The machine cannot heat water during temperature-controlled wash cycles.
What You Can Check: Ensure the selected program includes a warm or hot wash setting.
Possible Service Requirement: The internal heating element, NTC temperature thermistor, or PCB heater relay needs electrical multimeter testing.
E50 Motor drive fault.
Possible Reason: The electronic control module detects abnormal motor rotation or drive overload.
What You Can Check: Turn off the power for 10 minutes and check that the drum turns smoothly by hand when empty.
Possible Service Requirement: A technician inspects the drive motor coils, inverter board components, or drive belt.

Important Note on Error Codes: Error codes can vary between models and series. The same code may not mean exactly the same thing on every washing machine. Share the exact model number and displayed code before booking so the problem can be checked correctly.
