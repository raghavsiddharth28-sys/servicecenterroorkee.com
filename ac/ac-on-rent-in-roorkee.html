Q: AC delivery aur installation mein kitna time lagta hai?

Ans: Booking confirm hone aur basic verification ke baad usually 24 se 48 hours ke andar doorstep delivery aur professional installation complete kar di jaati hai.

Q: Kya rental AC achhi working condition mein hota hai?

Q: Kya commercial offices ya shops ke liye bulk AC on rent milte hain?

Ans: Haan, temporary commercial projects, site offices aur showrooms ke liye multiple units (2 to 5 units) bulk rental plans par provide ki jaati hain.
