Frequently Asked Questions About AC Repair in Roorkee

Q: AC chal raha hai par room mein cooling bilkul nahi ho rahi, iska kya reason ho sakta hai?

Ans: Iske main technical reasons gas leakage (low refrigerant pressure), weak starting capacitor jiski vajah se compressor start nahi le raha, ya outdoor condenser coil par heavy dust blockage ho sakte hain. Doorstep technician manifold gauge se refrigerant pressure aur multimeter se capacitor microfarad check karke exact fault diagnose karte hain.

Q: AC indoor unit se pani tapakne ka kya permanent solution hai?

Ans: Water leakage aam taur par condensate drain pipe mein dirt/algae jamne se ya indoor backplate slope kharab hone se hoti hai. Technician high-pressure flush se drain pipe clear karte hain, drain tray crack inspect karte hain aur proper 5-degree gravity drainage slope align karte hain.

Q: AC on karne par ghar ka MCB kyun trip ho jata hai?

Ans: MCB trip hona severe electrical short circuit ya locked rotor amps (LRA) ki nishani hai. Ye compressor winding short hone, outdoor fan motor stator burn hone ya loose terminal wiring sparking ki wajah se hota hai. Technician insulation resistance test karke faulty component identify karte hain.

Q: Inverter AC mein error code (jaise E1, E6, F3) aane par kya karna chahiye?

Ans: Inverter AC displays diagnostic error codes flash karte hain jo indoor-outdoor communication failure, temperature sensor drift ya outdoor PCB power module (IPM) overload indicate karte hain. Technician specific error chart decode karke sensor resistance aur circuit voltage test karte hain.

Q: Kya Inverter AC ka PCB board repair ho sakta hai ya new lagwana padta hai?

Ans: Inverter PCB par minor power supply faults, blown capacitors, fuse aur voltage regulator diode faults board-level repair ke through restore ho jaate hain. Lekin agar main microcontroller chip burn ho jaye ya PCB tracks burnt hon, toh safe operation ke liye new board replacement recommend kiya jaata hai.

Q: Split AC ke thin copper pipe par baraf (ice) kyun jamne lagti hai?

Ans: Discharge copper pipe par baraf jamna system mein gas leakage ka sabse bada symptom hai. Refrigerant pressure drop hone par boiling point negative mein chala jaata hai jisse moisture freeze ho jaati hai. System leak repair karke vacuumization ke baad gas charging required hoti hai.

Q: Roorkee mein AC repair visit aur inspection charge kitna hai?

Ans: Roorkee mein doorstep AC inspection aur diagnostic visit fee ₹199 hai. Technician aapke location par complete electrical testing aur fault isolate karte hain, aur kaam shuru karne se pehle clear estimate share karte hain.

Q: AC capacitor change karne ka kharcha kitna aata hai?

Ans: Standard split aur window ACs ke liye dual running capacitor replacement cost usually ₹450 se ₹850 ke beech aati hai, depending on MFD rating (35 MFD, 45 MFD ya 55 MFD) aur brand quality.

Q: AC gas charge karne se pehle nitrogen test kyun zaroori hai?

Ans: Bina leakage trace kiye gas charge karne se naye gas bhi 2–3 din mein udd sakti hai. Hum pehle high-pressure nitrogen gas se 250–300 PSI pressure hold karke U-bends aur joints par soap test karte hain, copper brazing se leak seal karte hain aur fir vacuum karke gas charge karte hain.

Q: Kya outdoor condenser fan motor repair ho sakti hai?

Ans: Agar fan motor ka capacitor fail ho ya bush dry ho, toh servicing aur capacitor replacement se motor normal ho jaati hai. Agar winding jal chuki ho ya bearing seize ho, toh high-RPM compatible motor replacement recommend ki jaati hai.

Q: Kya aap R32 aur R410A inverter ACs ki gas charging handle karte hain?

Ans: Haan, hamare technicians R32 aur R410A eco-friendly refrigerants ke liye calibrated digital manifold gauges aur two-stage rotary vacuum pumps use karte hain, taaki moisture-free weighed gas charging ensure ho sake.

Q: AC compressor replace karwane ka decision kab lena chahiye?

Ans: Jab compressor ki internal mechanical pump valve break ho jaye (no pumping), terminal body earth short ho jaye ya internal piston jam ho jaye jise hard-start capacitor se bhi release na kiya ja sake, tabhi compressor replacement advise kiya jaata hai.

Q: Kya purane 8-10 saal purane AC models ke spare parts Roorkee mein mil jaate hain?

Ans: Haan, standard non-inverter aur legacy window ACs ke universal capacitors, thermostats, relays, aur blower motors easily source ho jaate hain. Specialized electronic boards availability check karke arrange kiye jaate hain.

Q: Kya Civil Lines aur Ramnagar mein same-day AC repair visit available hai?

Ans: Haan, Civil Lines, Ramnagar, Ganeshpur, IIT Campus aur nearby Roorkee localities mein fast doorstep response maintain kiya jaata hai. Technician common spares aur testing equipment ke sath reach karte hain.

Q: AC remote se command nahi le raha hai par manual button se chalta hai?

Ans: Ye issue indoor display PCB par infrared (IR) receiver sensor diode fail hone ya remote transmitter glitch hone se hota hai. Technician IR sensor replace karke ya universal remote configure karke control restore karte hain.

Q: Window AC aur Split AC repair cost mein kya difference hota hai?

Ans: Window AC compact single-chassis hota hai isliye dismantling aur labor charges usually lower rehte hain. Split AC mein indoor aur outdoor units separate hone aur outdoor accessibility/bracket working ki vajah se labor aur piping checkup involvement zyada hoti hai.

Q: AC repair ke baad cooling ka checkup kaise kiya jaata hai?

Ans: Repair complete hone ke baad digital anemometer aur thermometer se return air aur supply air ka delta-T (temperature difference of 8–10°C) verify kiya jaata hai, aur clamp meter se continuous amp draw test kiya jaata hai.

Q: Roorkee mein doorstep AC repair book karne ka kya process hai?

Ans: Aap hamare helpline number +91 92115 12088 par direct call kar sakte hain ya WhatsApp message drop kar sakte hain. Apna AC brand, type (Split/Window/Inverter), aur issue share karein, convenient time slot par technician visit confirm ho jaayegi.
