Comprehensive AC General Maintenance & Checkup

Hamari seasonal servicing mein standard superficial dust dusting nahi ki jaati. Technician ek complete 12-point operational checklist execute karte hain taaki AC summer mein continuous fault-free cooling provide kare:

1. Airflow & Blower Inspection Cross-flow blower par accumulated dirt weight check kiya jaata hai jisse motor load badhta hai aur air throw restrict hoti hai.
2. Electrical Amp Draw Check Digital clamp meter se compressor aur fan motor ka running current test kiya jaata hai taaki over-amperage heating prevent ho sake.
3. Condensate Drain Line Flushing Drain pan aur drain pipe mein chemical flush pressure se algae aur biofilm saaf kiya jaata hai taaki indoor leakage zero rahe.
4. Temperature Differential (Delta-T) Room return air aur supply air grille temperature measure karke 8°C–12°C ka healthy cooling drop verify kiya jaata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions About AC Service in Roorkee

Q: AC ko saal mein kitni baar service karwana chahiye?

Ans: Roorkee ke high-dust climate mein AC ko saal mein kam se kam 2 baar service karwana recommend kiya jaata hai: ek baar summer shuru hone se pehle (March–April mein) full jet wash, aur ek baar mid-season (July–August humidity ke dauran) drain aur filter cleaning.

Q: Jet pump AC service aur normal dry service mein kya fark hai?

Ans: Normal dry service mein sirf mesh filters aur outer cover ko brush se saaf kiya jaata hai. Jet pump service mein waterproof jacket lagakar high-pressure water spray se internal cooling fins aur cross-flow blower ke andar phansi mitti aur fungus ko deep wash kiya jaata hai.

Q: Kya jet service ke dauran dewar ya furniture par paani girta hai?

Ans: Bilkul nahi. Hamare technicians specialized waterproof service jacket (catch apron) use karte hain jo indoor unit ko completely seal kar leti hai aur sara wash water pipe ke through bucket mein safely collect ho jaata hai.

Q: Regular AC service se electricity bill par kya effect padta hai?

Ans: Clogged coils aur chocked blower ki vajah se compressor ko heat transfer karne ke liye zyada der tak chalna padta hai. Proper jet cleaning heat dissipation improve karti hai jisse compressor jaldi cut-off leta hai aur electricity consumption 15–25% tak kam ho sakti hai.

Q: AC on karte hi aane wali bad smell (musty odor) kaise hat-ti hai?

Ans: Ye smell evaporator coil aur drain pan par jamne wali mold aur bacterial biofilm ki vajah se aati hai. Foam deep sanitization service mein anti-bacterial active foam apply karke coil fins aur drain tray ko sanitize kiya jaata hai jisse smell completely khatam ho jaati hai.

Q: Roorkee mein split AC service ka standard charge kitna hai?

Ans: Basic standard AC service ₹299 se shuru hoti hai, jabki full indoor aur outdoor high-pressure jet pump service ₹499–₹750 ke beech provide ki jaati hai depending on accessibility aur cleaning level.

Q: Kya outdoor condensing unit ko bhi jet wash kiya jaata hai?

Ans: Haan, outdoor condenser fins par mitti jamne se compressor ka discharge pressure dangerously badh jaata hai jisse AC bar-bar cut-off karta hai. Safe electrical protection ke sath outdoor coil par high-pressure jet wash perform kiya jaata hai.

Q: Inverter AC ki servicing ke dauran outdoor PCB safe kaise rehti hai?

Ans: Inverter ACs mein outdoor unit ke top par microcontroller board hota hai. Technician pehle electrical casing par waterproof protective cover place karte hain aur water jet sirf lower condenser fins par direct karte hain, taaki zero moisture contact rahe.

Q: Kya AC service mein gas checkup bhi include hota hai?

Ans: Haan, service ke end mein technician temperature differential aur cooling line pressure check karte hain. Agar cooling normal range mein hai, unnecessary gas charging avoid ki jaati hai.

Q: Window AC ki service kaise perform ki jaati hai?

Ans: Window AC ko wooden/metal frame se carefully slide-out kiya jaata hai aur dedicated wash area mein le jaakar base pan ke sludge ko clean karke double-shaft motor aur fins ko jet wash karke dry hone ke baad refit kiya jaata hai.

Q: Service ke kitni der baad AC on kar sakte hain?

Ans: Technician unit ko dry blower se wipe-down karke basic test run on-the-spot perform karte hain. Usually service complete hone ke 15–20 minutes baad AC normally regular use ke liye ready hota hai.

Q: Kya drainage pipe ki cleaning service mein shamil hoti hai?

Ans: Haan, drain line ko high-pressure water pump se reverse aur forward flush kiya jaata hai taaki andar phanse algae clots clear ho sakein aur indoor water overflow na ho.

Q: Kya purane ACs ki chemical cleaning safe hoti hai?

Ans: Hum neutral pH aur non-corrosive eco-friendly coil sanitizers use karte hain jo copper tubing aur aluminum fins ko corrode nahi karte, balki sirf dust aur oil film ko safely rinse off karte hain.

Q: Kya multiple ACs (jaise 3 ya 4 units) ki service par package discount milta hai?

Q: Service aur repair mein kya difference hota hai?
