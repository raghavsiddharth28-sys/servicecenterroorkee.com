AC Installation Types We Handle in Roorkee

Har air conditioner type ka mounting structure aur refrigeration interconnect alag hota hai. Hum standard safety norms ke according fitting karte hain:

Split AC Installation in Roorkee

Indoor unit backplate ko spirit level se align karke high-grip anchor fasteners se secure kiya jaata hai. Core drill se wall hole cut kiya jaata hai jisme copper pipes aur drain line smoothly bahar pass hote hain. Outdoor unit ko heavy-gauge angle iron bracket par bolt kiya jaata hai with vibration dampers.

Includes: Indoor Backplate Mount + Outdoor Bracket Fixing + Piping Connect + Leak Test

Inverter AC Installation & Deep Vacuuming

Inverter air conditioners (R32 aur R410A gas) moisture aur atmospheric air ke liye extremely sensitive hote hain. Hamare technicians rotary vacuum pump se pure connecting circuit ko down to 500 microns vacuumize karte hain BEFORE releasing refrigerant, jisse non-condensable gases remove hoti hain aur compressor tripping prevent hoti hai.

Mandatory Protocol: Two-Stage Vacuumization + Torque Wrench Flare Tightening

Window AC Installation in Roorkee

Window AC ko wooden frame ya concrete sill par secure mounting di jaati hai. Outer chassis ko 3–5 degree backward tilt par fix kiya jaata hai taaki condensate water bahar drip ho sake aur kamre ke andar leak na kare. Side gaps ko insulation foam aur acrylic sheet se air-tight seal kiya jaata hai.

Includes: Frame Leveling + Rear Slope Alignment + Air Gap Sealing

AC Uninstallation & Relocation Service

House shifting ya room change ke time AC uninstallation professional pump-down method se kiya jaata hai. Technician system ki refrigerant gas ko outdoor condenser coil ke andar lock (pump-down) kar dete hain taaki zero gas loss ho, aur copper pipes ko dust-proof cap karke safe pack kiya jaata hai.

Includes: 100% Gas Pump-Down Lock + Safe Dismantling + Bracket Removal

Step-by-Step Professional AC Installation Process

Hamare certified technicians step-by-step engineering guidelines follow karte hain taaki noise, leakage aur vibration free operation mile:

Step 1: Site & Wall Inspection Room size, sun exposure aur load-bearing wall strength evaluate karke indoor-outdoor placement decide ki jaati hai.
Step 2: Indoor Plate Leveling Magnetic spirit level se backplate ko perfectly horizontal fix kiya jaata hai with slight gravity slope towards drain exit.
Step 3: Circular Core Hole Cutting 3-inch diamond core drill bit se clean circular hole banaya jaata hai taaki copper pipes bina kisi sharp bend ke bahar niklein.
Step 4: Heavy Outdoor Bracket Bolting Heavy-gauge powder-coated MS brackets ko solid anchor bolts se external wall par mount kiya jaata hai with anti-vibration pads.
Step 5: Copper Line Flaring & Torque Precision eccentric flaring tool se 45° smooth flare face banayi jaati hai aur torque wrench se flare nuts seal kiye jaate hain.
Step 6: Vacuuming & Commissioning Rotary vacuum pump se air aur moisture nikaali jaati hai, bubble leak test pass hone par refrigerant release karke cooling test hoti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions About AC Installation in Roorkee

Q: Inverter AC installation ke time vacuuming karwana kyun zaroori hota hai?
