Common Livpure Kitchen Chimney Problems & Solutions

Detailed breakdown of genuine issues faced by Livpure chimney owners in Roorkee kitchens with root causes and repair methods.

1 Livpure Auto-Clean Cycle Not Collecting Oil

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows livpure auto-clean cycle not collecting oil.

Possible Reason: Blocked oil drainage channel or malfunctioning heating pad resistor.

What Technician Checks: Checks drain spout clearance and measures heating element resistance with meter.

Possible Solution: Clearing oil conduit, descaling drain aperture, or replacing heating coil pad.

2 Livpure Wave Sensor Turning ON Unexpectedly

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows livpure wave sensor turning on unexpectedly.

Possible Reason: Light interference, optical lens greasy, or damaged sensor sensitivity circuit.

What Technician Checks: Inspects sensor calibration and cleans front optic window.

Possible Solution: Adjusting sensor sensitivity, cleaning optic lens, or replacing sensor PCB.

3 Suction Dropping on Livpure Urbane Chimney

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows suction dropping on livpure urbane chimney.

Possible Reason: Baffle filters choked with hardened cooking fat and spices.

What Technician Checks: Tests airflow velocity at intake and checks exhaust air pressure.

Possible Solution: Caustic degreasing bath for baffle filters or ₹850 full deep cleaning.

4 Livpure Motor Humming Without Rotation

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows livpure motor humming without rotation.

Possible Reason: Blown motor starting capacitor or seized bronze sleeve bearings.

What Technician Checks: Checks capacitor microfarad reading and manually rotates impeller shaft.

Possible Solution: Installing fresh heavy-duty motor capacitor or lubricating motor shaft.

5 Livpure Chimney Completely Dead (No Lights, No Fan)

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows livpure chimney completely dead (no lights, no fan).

Possible Reason: Blown cartridge fuse on power supply board or disconnected neutral line.

What Technician Checks: Tests 230V AC input voltage and traces circuit on primary power board.

Possible Solution: Replacing micro-fuse, fixing wiring terminal, or swapping power supply board.

6 Excessive Vibration at High Speed Setting

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows excessive vibration at high speed setting.

Possible Reason: Broken impeller blade vane or unbalanced grease collection on blower fan.

What Technician Checks: Inspects centrifugal fan wheel for missing or chipped plastic/metal fins.

Possible Solution: Scraping fan grease buildup evenly or installing replacement blower wheel.

7 Touch Panel Display Shows Error or Beeps Constantly

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows touch panel display shows error or beeps constantly.

Possible Reason: Capacitive membrane shorted by condensed moisture behind tempered glass.

What Technician Checks: Disassembles front glass panel and checks touch PCB for corrosion.

Possible Solution: Drying touch board, replacing damaged ribbon connector, or replacing touch panel.

8 Oil Dropping Down onto Gas Stove

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows oil dropping down onto gas stove.

Possible Reason: Overfilled oil cup or broken silicone seal along chimney collection rim.

What Technician Checks: Inspects oil cup latch and silicone gasket around collector ring.

Possible Solution: Emptying reservoir, replacing damaged collector cup, and resealing rim.

9 Exhaust Duct Emitting Loud Flapping Sound

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows exhaust duct emitting loud flapping sound.

Possible Reason: Damper flap hinges loose or flap fluttering against high outdoor wind.

What Technician Checks: Inspects exterior wall vent outlet flap.

Possible Solution: Securing damper pivot pin and fitting weighted aerodynamic one-way flap.

10 LED Lights Dim or Dead

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows led lights dim or dead.

Possible Reason: Defective 12V LED driver transformer or loose solder connection.

What Technician Checks: Measures DC voltage from driver module to LED terminals.

Possible Solution: Replacing faulty LED driver module or installing high-brightness LED units.

11 Smoke Spilling Back into Kitchen

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows smoke spilling back into kitchen.

Possible Reason: Flexible exhaust pipe crimped or choked by bird nest at wall termination.

What Technician Checks: Inspects full length of aluminium duct and exterior outlet louvers.

Possible Solution: Clearing exterior blockage, straightening duct run, and securing vent cowl.

12 Motor Emitting Burning Odor

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows motor emitting burning odor.

Possible Reason: Overheating copper winding or electrical insulation breakdown.

What Technician Checks: Measures winding resistance across speeds with insulation tester.

Possible Solution: Motor rewinding or replacement of genuine Livpure blower motor.

13 Push Button Unit Loose or Not Locking Speed

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows push button unit loose or not locking speed.

Possible Reason: Broken latching clip inside mechanical switch bank.

What Technician Checks: Tests mechanical spring detent mechanism.

Possible Solution: Replacing 3-speed mechanical switch assembly.

14 Auto-Clean Button LED Blinking Continuously

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows auto-clean button led blinking continuously.

Possible Reason: Controller notifying service interval timeout or thermal sensor error.

What Technician Checks: Checks MCU error register and resets maintenance timer.

Possible Solution: Clearing service timer count and performing thermal self-test.

15 Chimney Metal Body Giving Minor Shock

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows chimney metal body giving minor shock.

Possible Reason: Inadequate domestic earthing or internal phase wire touching chassis.

What Technician Checks: Measures ground-to-earth potential with digital multi-meter.

Possible Solution: Connecting solid chassis earthing wire and insulating internal terminals.

16 Air Suction Leaking from Duct Collar Joint

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows air suction leaking from duct collar joint.

Possible Reason: Torn duct tape or loose hose clamp at exhaust connection.

What Technician Checks: Smokes test at collar connection point to detect air bypass.

Possible Solution: Re-clamping duct collar with steel jubilee clip and sealing with aluminium foil tape.

17 Chimney Turning OFF Suddenly After 10 Minutes

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows chimney turning off suddenly after 10 minutes.

Possible Reason: Thermal overload protector (TOP) opening due to inadequate motor cooling.

What Technician Checks: Monitors motor surface temperature during operation.

Possible Solution: Cleaning motor cooling fins and verifying correct supply voltage.

18 Livpure Straight Line Mesh Filters Torn

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows livpure straight line mesh filters torn.

Possible Reason: Corrosion and rough washing damaging thin aluminium mesh layers.

What Technician Checks: Inspects mesh frame for punctures.

Possible Solution: Supplying replacement multi-layer washable aluminium cassette filters.

19 Glass Canopy Loosened or Vibrating Loudly

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows glass canopy loosened or vibrating loudly.

Possible Reason: Mounting hardware worked loose from wall due to vibration.

What Technician Checks: Checks wall anchor integrity with hex wrench.

Possible Solution: Retightening glass clamping brackets with silicone anti-vibration gaskets.

20 Sluggish Air Intake Despite Motor at High Speed

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows sluggish air intake despite motor at high speed.

Possible Reason: Exhaust duct run has excessive 90-degree elbows creating heavy backpressure.

What Technician Checks: Measures static pressure inside exhaust pipeline.

Possible Solution: Re-routing ducting to minimize sharp elbows and reduce pipe resistance.

21 Oil Staining Inside Wall Behind Chimney

Problem / Symptom: Livpure chimney shows oil staining inside wall behind chimney.

Possible Reason: Improper gap between chimney rear flange and tiled kitchen wall.

What Technician Checks: Inspects rear sealing bead along tile boundary.

Possible Solution: Applying heat-resistant silicone sealant along back wall interface.
