What is Covered in Professional Kitchen Chimney Installation

Our technicians follow a comprehensive checklist during doorstep installation to ensure complete safety and long-term durability:

01. Optimal Height & Level Alignment

Installed at strictly 65cm to 75cm (26–30 inches) above the gas cooktop for optimal suction efficiency and fire safety.

02. Wall Bracket Mounting

Heavy-duty wall anchors drilled with spirit-level precision to eliminate motor vibration and structural rattling.

03. Exhaust Duct Pipe Routing

Installation of flexible expandable 6-inch aluminum ducting with minimum bends to minimize air resistance and blower noise.

04. Outer Cowl / Louver Installation

Weather-resistant external wall cap fitted with mesh grill to prevent rainwater, dust, and birds from entering the duct.

05. Touch / Gesture Sensor & Motor Suction Check

Full speed testing across all airflow levels, auto-clean heating verification, and gesture control calibration.
