Common BlowHot Kitchen Chimney Problems & Solutions

Detailed breakdown of genuine issues faced by BlowHot chimney owners in Roorkee kitchens with root causes and repair methods.

1 BlowHot Ariel Auto-Clean Not Melting Oil

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows blowhot ariel auto-clean not melting oil.

Possible Reason: Heating resistor coil burnt out or relay on main PCB burned.

What Technician Checks: Tests heating element resistance and checks 230V relay trigger line.

Possible Solution: Replacing heating element pad or repairing relay on main PCB.

2 BlowHot Wave Sensor Not Detecting Hand Motion

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows blowhot wave sensor not detecting hand motion.

Possible Reason: Cooking oil film covering sensor window or loose optical ribbon cable.

What Technician Checks: Tests sensor voltage transition across hand wave with multimeter.

Possible Solution: Cleaning sensor window or replacing gesture sensor daughterboard.

3 Weak Air Suction on BlowHot Sapphire Chimney

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows weak air suction on blowhot sapphire chimney.

Possible Reason: Baffle filters clogged with hardened grease and mustard oil residues.

What Technician Checks: Measures airflow velocity at filter face with digital anemometer.

Possible Solution: Caustic degreasing bath for filters or ₹850 complete deep cleaning.

4 Motor Humming Loudly But Impeller Not Spinning

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows motor humming loudly but impeller not spinning.

Possible Reason: Motor run capacitor degraded or motor bearings seized due to grease.

What Technician Checks: Tests capacitor microfarad rating and manually rotates impeller shaft.

Possible Solution: Installing new 5uF/6uF capacitor and lubricating motor shaft bushings.

5 BlowHot Chimney Not Turning ON (Completely Dead)

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows blowhot chimney not turning on (completely dead).

Possible Reason: Blown mains glass fuse or disconnected internal power harness.

What Technician Checks: Traces 230V AC supply to PCB input terminals and checks DC rails.

Possible Solution: Replacing onboard fuse, repairing power line, or replacing power PCB.

6 Excessive Vibration at High Speed Setting

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows excessive vibration at high speed setting.

Possible Reason: Centrifugal blower fan wheel has missing balance weights or grease cake.

What Technician Checks: Inspects blower rotor for damage or uneven grease build-up.

Possible Solution: Degreasing fan wheel blades thoroughly and re-balancing.

7 Oil Leaking Out of BlowHot Oil Collector Cup

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows oil leaking out of blowhot oil collector cup.

Possible Reason: Oil collector cup misaligned, full, or silicone drain seal torn.

What Technician Checks: Examines slope of drain channel and checks collector cup latch.

Possible Solution: Emptying reservoir, replacing silicone seal, and realigning cup.

8 Touch Panel Display Freezing or Beeping Unprompted

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows touch panel display freezing or beeping unprompted.

Possible Reason: Capacitive membrane shorted by condensed moisture behind glass.

What Technician Checks: Checks touch pad continuity and inspects flat ribbon cable.

Possible Solution: Drying touch board, replacing ribbon cable, or replacing touch panel.

9 Exhaust Smoke Leaking Back into Kitchen

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows exhaust smoke leaking back into kitchen.

Possible Reason: Aluminium duct pipe torn or exterior backdraft flap jammed shut.

What Technician Checks: Inspects full duct run from chimney collar to outside wall.

Possible Solution: Clearing exterior blockage, unjamming flap, and sealing duct with foil tape.

10 LED Lighting Flickering or Completely Dead

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows led lighting flickering or completely dead.

Possible Reason: Defective constant current LED driver transformer or loose solder.

What Technician Checks: Measures DC voltage at LED lamp terminal connectors.

Possible Solution: Replacing 12V LED driver module or installing high-lumen LED spots.

11 Push Button Assembly Jammed on BlowHot Sapphire

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows push button assembly jammed on blowhot sapphire.

Possible Reason: Sticky cooking grease penetrated into mechanical switch sliders.

What Technician Checks: Checks switch mechanical latching action and contact resistance.

Possible Solution: Degreasing switch contacts or replacing 3-speed keypad block.

12 Chimney Tripping Home Circuit Breaker (MCB)

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows chimney tripping home circuit breaker (mcb).

Possible Reason: Stator winding shorted to ground or heating element insulation breakdown.

What Technician Checks: Performs megger insulation test on motor and heating circuit.

Possible Solution: Motor rewinding or replacing defective auto-clean heating band.

13 Blower Running at Only One Single Speed

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows blower running at only one single speed.

Possible Reason: Defective triac speed controller on PCB or tapped winding burnt.

What Technician Checks: Probes triac gating signals on PCB across speed settings.

Possible Solution: Replacing speed control triac or installing new control board.

14 Burning Plastic Smell from Chimney Body

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows burning plastic smell from chimney body.

Possible Reason: Overheated motor winding or wire insulation touching hot metal casing.

What Technician Checks: Thermal inspection of motor stator housing and wiring harness.

Possible Solution: Power shut-off, wire harness insulation, or motor replacement if burnt.

15 Rattling Noise Inside Exhaust Duct Collar

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows rattling noise inside exhaust duct collar.

Possible Reason: One-way exhaust damper flap vibrating loose under high air discharge.

What Technician Checks: Inspects discharge adapter flap hinge and spring tension.

Possible Solution: Securing damper pivot pin and fitting vibration felt buffer.

16 Chimney Automatically Shuts Off After 10 Minutes

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows chimney automatically shuts off after 10 minutes.

Possible Reason: Thermal overload protector (TOP) opening due to inadequate motor cooling.

What Technician Checks: Measures motor temperature rise during continuous operation.

Possible Solution: Clearing choked motor cooling vents and checking voltage supply.

17 Mild Electric Shock on Touching Chimney Metal

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows mild electric shock on touching chimney metal.

Possible Reason: Improper socket earthing or neutral leakage to metal chassis.

What Technician Checks: Measures chassis voltage to earth with digital multimeter.

Possible Solution: Restoring three-pin chassis ground bond and insulating joints.

18 Stagnant Oil Smell Coming from Chimney Vents

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows stagnant oil smell coming from chimney vents.

Possible Reason: Rancid oil accumulation in inner duct collar and blower housing.

What Technician Checks: Visual endoscopy of blower snail casing.

Possible Solution: Complete ₹850 industrial chemical wash and sanitization.

19 Glass Canopy Loosened from Wall Brackets

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows glass canopy loosened from wall brackets.

Possible Reason: Wall anchor fasteners loosened under years of motor vibration.

What Technician Checks: Inspects tempered glass support bracket clamps with wrench.

Possible Solution: Tightening wall anchors and fitting rubber vibration washers.

20 Duct Collar Dislodged from Chimney Spigot

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows duct collar dislodged from chimney spigot.

Possible Reason: Vibration loosened sheet metal duct clamp.

What Technician Checks: Checks exhaust collar flange fitment.

Possible Solution: Re-attaching collar with steel screws and aluminium foil tape.

21 Reduced Suction Despite Clean Baffle Filters

Problem / Symptom: BlowHot chimney shows reduced suction despite clean baffle filters.

Possible Reason: Exhaust duct run has excessive 90-degree bends creating backpressure.

What Technician Checks: Calculates static backpressure in duct line.

Possible Solution: Re-routing flexible duct to straightest path and reducing bend angles.
