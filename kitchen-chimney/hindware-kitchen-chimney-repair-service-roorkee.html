Common Hindware Kitchen Chimney Problems & Solutions

Detailed breakdown of genuine issues faced by Hindware chimney owners in Roorkee kitchens with root causes and repair methods.

1 Hindware Nadia Motion Sensor Not Responding

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware nadia motion sensor not responding.

Possible Reason: Grease film covering infrared optic lenses or optical sensor PCB ribbon cable loose.

What Technician Checks: Cleans optical window, tests IR emitter-receiver voltage with digital multimeter.

Possible Solution: Sensor calibration, lens cleaning, or replacement of motion sensor PCB unit.

2 Hindware Nevio Auto-Clean Not Melting Oil

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware nevio auto-clean not melting oil.

Possible Reason: Thermal heating coil open-circuit or auto-clean relay on main control board burned.

What Technician Checks: Tests heating element resistance (ohms) and checks 230V relay trigger line.

Possible Solution: Replacing heating coil pad or restoring PCB relay terminal contacts.

3 Weak Suction on Hindware Filterless Models

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows weak suction on hindware filterless models.

Possible Reason: Centrifugal impeller blades loaded with hardened oil sludge reducing aerodynamic lift.

What Technician Checks: Measures airflow CFM at hood intake and inspects internal blower drum.

Possible Solution: Complete dismounting and industrial chemical deep cleaning (₹850).

4 Loud Vibrating Noise from Hindware Blower

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows loud vibrating noise from hindware blower.

Possible Reason: Foreign object lodged in impeller, dry motor shaft bearings, or broken motor clamp.

What Technician Checks: Spins blower wheel manually to check eccentric run-out and bearing play.

Possible Solution: Lubricating sintered bronze bushings, balancing impeller, or replacing mounting rubbers.

5 Oil Dripping from Hindware Glass Edges

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows oil dripping from hindware glass edges.

Possible Reason: Full oil collector trough, broken oil drain channel gasket, or improper wall tilt.

What Technician Checks: Inspects slope gradient with spirit level and examines silicone sealing bead.

Possible Solution: Emptying reservoir, replacing collector channel seals, and leveling wall brackets.

6 Hindware Chimney Not Turning ON

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware chimney not turning on.

Possible Reason: Blown glass cartridge fuse on power supply board or severed main line power cord.

What Technician Checks: Checks mains voltage at wall socket and traces input power to power PCB terminal.

Possible Solution: Replacing blown micro-fuse, rewiring power cord, or fixing power PCB.

7 Hindware Touch Panel Freezing or Beeping Repeatedly

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware touch panel freezing or beeping repeatedly.

Possible Reason: Capacitive touch panel moisture ingress or micro-controller hung due to voltage surge.

What Technician Checks: Isolates front glass capacitive ribbon and checks 5V DC regulated output.

Possible Solution: Drying capacitive sensor pad, resetting firmware, or installing new touch switch assembly.

8 Motor Humming But Impeller Not Rotating

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows motor humming but impeller not rotating.

Possible Reason: Failed motor run capacitor (typically 4uF to 6uF) or seized motor sleeve bearings.

What Technician Checks: Measures capacitance with digital meter and checks motor shaft rotation freedom.

Possible Solution: Installing fresh heavy-duty motor capacitor or repairing seized motor shaft.

9 Smoke Spilling Back into Kitchen

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows smoke spilling back into kitchen.

Possible Reason: Exterior duct flap jammed shut or flexible aluminium duct pipe torn or pinched.

What Technician Checks: Inspects outdoor gravity louvers and checks duct run across kitchen false ceiling.

Possible Solution: Unjamming external outlet flap and resealing aluminium duct joints with foil tape.

10 LED Strip Lights Flickering or Dead

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows led strip lights flickering or dead.

Possible Reason: Defective constant current LED driver transformer or loose solder joints on LED bar.

What Technician Checks: Measures 12V DC output from transformer to LED array.

Possible Solution: Replacing faulty LED driver module or installing high-lumen waterproof LED strip.

11 Hindware Speed Selector Buttons Stuck

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware speed selector buttons stuck.

Possible Reason: Oil and dirt gumming up mechanical switch contacts inside push-button box.

What Technician Checks: Disassembles switch assembly and checks spring return mechanism.

Possible Solution: Degreasing switch contacts or replacing multi-position push button cluster.

12 Chimney Tripping Home MCB Circuit Breaker

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows chimney tripping home mcb circuit breaker.

Possible Reason: Electrical short circuit in motor stator winding or heating coil insulation breakdown.

What Technician Checks: Performs megger insulation test between active leads and chassis ground.

Possible Solution: Rewinding motor stator coil or replacing defective heating band.

13 Blower Running at Only One Single Speed

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows blower running at only one single speed.

Possible Reason: Tapped winding resistor burned or failed triac speed controller on PCB.

What Technician Checks: Probes triac gating signals on PCB across Low, Medium, and High settings.

Possible Solution: Replacing speed triac components or replacing built-in motor PCB.

14 Burning Plastic Smell from Chimney Canopy

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows burning plastic smell from chimney canopy.

Possible Reason: Overheated motor run winding, melting wire insulation, or stuck blower dragging motor.

What Technician Checks: Thermal inspection of motor stator housing and wiring harness.

Possible Solution: Immediate power shut-off, wire harness repair, and motor replacement if burned.

15 Auto-Clean Cycle Shuts Off Immediately

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows auto-clean cycle shuts off immediately.

Possible Reason: Defective timer IC on PCB or thermal cut-out switch opening prematurely.

What Technician Checks: Times auto-clean activation voltage and checks thermal limit switch.

Possible Solution: Replacing PCB timer relay or resetting thermal limit switch.

16 Heavy Vibration Shaking Kitchen Cabinetry

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows heavy vibration shaking kitchen cabinetry.

Possible Reason: Wall bracket fasteners loose in brick masonry or missing anti-vibration rubber pads.

What Technician Checks: Checks wall anchor torque and cabinet clearance gap.

Possible Solution: Drilling heavy-duty wall anchors and installing vibration damping pads.

17 Hindware Oil Collector Cup Cracked or Missing

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware oil collector cup cracked or missing.

Possible Reason: Thermal stress or physical impact during DIY cleaning.

What Technician Checks: Measures model mounting clip dimensions.

Possible Solution: Supplying genuine replacement tempered plastic or stainless steel oil cup.

18 Suction Dropping After Monsoon

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows suction dropping after monsoon.

Possible Reason: High humidity causing dust and flour to cake onto grease-coated blower blades.

What Technician Checks: Visual endoscopy of impeller interior.

Possible Solution: Deep degreasing service (₹850) with non-corrosive surfactant foam.

19 Hindware Duct Outlet Rattling in High Wind

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows hindware duct outlet rattling in high wind.

Possible Reason: Lightweight plastic exhaust flap slapping against hood body during windy days.

What Technician Checks: Examines external wall vent collar.

Possible Solution: Fitting spring-loaded weighted one-way damper flap.

20 Touch Controls Triggering Automatically

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows touch controls triggering automatically.

Possible Reason: Cooking steam condensing on exterior glass touch surface mimicking finger touch.

What Technician Checks: Tests touch membrane sensitivity and grounding path.

Possible Solution: Ensuring proper chassis grounding and cleaning capacitive touch sensor plate.

21 Low Air Suction Despite Clean Baffle Filters

Problem / Symptom: Hindware chimney shows low air suction despite clean baffle filters.

Possible Reason: Exhaust duct run exceeding recommended 10-foot length or having too many 90-degree bends.

What Technician Checks: Calculates static backpressure in duct line.

Possible Solution: Re-routing flexible duct to straightest path and reducing bend angles.
