Common Faber Kitchen Chimney Problems & Solutions

Detailed breakdown of genuine issues faced by Faber chimney owners in Roorkee kitchens with root causes and repair methods.

1 Faber 3D Suction Dropping Drastically

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber 3d suction dropping drastically.

Possible Reason: Side air intake slots and baffle filters choked with solidified oil grease.

What Technician Checks: Measures air velocity with digital anemometer and checks filter clearance.

Possible Solution: Industrial chemical degreasing (₹850 deep clean) and side vent descaling.

2 Faber Auto-Clean Function Not Melting Oil

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber auto-clean function not melting oil.

Possible Reason: Internal thermal heating coil element open circuit or PCB auto-clean relay burnt.

What Technician Checks: Tests heating element resistance (ohms) and checks 230V trigger from PCB.

Possible Solution: Replace auto-clean heating strip coil or repair PCB relay circuit.

3 Faber Touch & Gesture Sensor Not Responding

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber touch & gesture sensor not responding.

Possible Reason: Oily film over optical sensor eyes or ribbon cable loose from main PCB.

What Technician Checks: Cleans optical sensor lens and checks 5V DC control voltage from board.

Possible Solution: Clean optical sensors, secure ribbon cable, or replace touch sensor board.

4 Loud Whistling or Rattling Sound from Blower

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows loud whistling or rattling sound from blower.

Possible Reason: Grease deposition on one side of Faber blower wheel causing dynamic unbalance.

What Technician Checks: Inspects blower impeller blades for grease sludge and checks shaft play.

Possible Solution: Disassemble blower, chemical wash impeller wheel, and balance motor shaft.

5 Oil Dripping from Faber Oil Collector Sides

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows oil dripping from faber oil collector sides.

Possible Reason: Oil collector canal choked with thick black grease or collector cup warped.

What Technician Checks: Checks slope angle of internal oil drainage canal and inspects cup latch.

Possible Solution: Clear grease canal with solvent steam and fit new heat-resistant oil cup.

6 Faber Chimney Motor Not Turning On (Lights Work)

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber chimney motor not turning on (lights work).

Possible Reason: Motor run capacitor degraded or thermal cut-out (TOP) tripped from overheating.

What Technician Checks: Tests capacitor capacitance (4µF-6µF) and checks motor coil continuity.

Possible Solution: Install fresh motor run capacitor and lubricate motor shaft bearings.

7 Faber Chimney Suddenly Switching Off Mid-Cooking

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber chimney suddenly switching off mid-cooking.

Possible Reason: Motor thermal overload protector kicking in due to heavy internal friction.

What Technician Checks: Measures motor temperature rise during high speed operation.

Possible Solution: Complete deep cleaning of blower assembly and check motor winding condition.

8 LED Cooking Spotlights Flickering or Dead

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows led cooking spotlights flickering or dead.

Possible Reason: Constant-current 12V LED driver module damaged by kitchen voltage spikes.

What Technician Checks: Measures DC voltage output from internal LED transformer driver.

Possible Solution: Replace 12V LED driver module and secure waterproof wire connectors.

9 Smoke Leaking Back Through Overhead Cabinets

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows smoke leaking back through overhead cabinets.

Possible Reason: Flexible aluminium duct pipe torn or loosely clamped on chimney collar.

What Technician Checks: Inspects exhaust duct pipe path inside cabinet loft.

Possible Solution: Re-clamp with heavy-duty metal jubilee clamp and seal with foil tape.

10 Motor Humming Loudly But Fan Stays Stationary

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows motor humming loudly but fan stays stationary.

Possible Reason: Motor bearings seized due to hot grease fumes drying up bearing lubricant.

What Technician Checks: Manually rotates motor shaft to detect mechanical binding or roughness.

Possible Solution: Clean motor shaft, free bearings with penetrating fluid, or replace capacitor.

11 Heavy Vibration Shaking Kitchen Wall Tiles

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows heavy vibration shaking kitchen wall tiles.

Possible Reason: Loose wall mounting bracket fasteners or broken blower fan fin.

What Technician Checks: Checks wall anchor anchor bolts and inspects blower impeller wheel integrity.

Possible Solution: Re-anchor wall bracket with heavy metal fasteners and balance blower.

12 Burning Smell from Faber Control Box

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows burning smell from faber control box.

Possible Reason: Loose spade terminal sparking under high current load or burnt relay coil.

What Technician Checks: Inspects PCB board terminals for charred plastic or carbon traces.

Possible Solution: Replace burnt terminal lugs, resolder copper tracks, or replace PCB.

13 Chimney Speed Buttons Jammed in Pressed State

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows chimney speed buttons jammed in pressed state.

Possible Reason: Cooking grease gummed up inside mechanical switch spring housing.

What Technician Checks: Opens switch bezel and checks spring mechanism movement.

Possible Solution: Degrease mechanical switch bank with contact cleaner or replace assembly.

14 Smoke Spreading into Living Room During Tadka

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows smoke spreading into living room during tadka.

Possible Reason: Exhaust duct pipe length exceeds 15 feet or has more than 3 sharp bends.

What Technician Checks: Evaluates duct routing resistance and outdoor louver opening angle.

Possible Solution: Optimize duct pipe path and clear external wall shutter flaps.

15 Faber Display Screen Showing Blinking Error

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber display screen showing blinking error.

Possible Reason: Microcontroller auto-clean timer count saturated or sensor feedback failure.

What Technician Checks: Performs factory reset sequence on Faber touch panel board.

Possible Solution: Reset maintenance timer or replace faulty temperature feedback sensor.

16 Aluminium Duct Pipe Crushed Behind Loft

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows aluminium duct pipe crushed behind loft.

Possible Reason: Weight of storage boxes in loft crushed thin foil duct pipe.

What Technician Checks: Inspects duct pipe cross-section area throughout the exhaust path.

Possible Solution: Replace with heavy-gauge flexible aluminium duct pipe.

17 Oil Leaking from Faber Baffle Filter Handles

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows oil leaking from faber baffle filter handles.

Possible Reason: Filters saturated beyond holding capacity; grease overflowing handles.

What Technician Checks: Checks baffle filter weight and saturation level.

Possible Solution: Deep chemical soaking bath (₹650 general service / ₹850 deep clean).

18 Motor Runs at Only One Low Speed

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows motor runs at only one low speed.

Possible Reason: Burnt speed tap relay on control board or speed selector switch fault.

What Technician Checks: Tests voltage across speed 1, 2, and 3 terminals on PCB.

Possible Solution: Replace faulty PCB motor speed relay or replace speed switch.

19 Outer Duct Louver Shutter Jammed Shut

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows outer duct louver shutter jammed shut.

Possible Reason: Sticky grease and dust glued outer plastic louvers shut on exterior wall.

What Technician Checks: Inspects outdoor exhaust cowl on external building wall.

Possible Solution: Degrease external louver flaps and ensure smooth gravity flap opening.

20 Faber Curved Glass Getting Extremely Hot

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber curved glass getting extremely hot.

Possible Reason: Chimney mounted too low or blower not pulling heat away efficiently.

What Technician Checks: Measures height between stovetop and chimney base (recommended 26-30 in).

Possible Solution: Adjust mounting height and restore full motor exhaust suction.

21 Faber Filterless Chimney Oil Shield Clogged

Problem / Symptom: Faber chimney shows faber filterless chimney oil shield clogged.

Possible Reason: Heavy cooking residue blocking centrifugal oil separation slots.

What Technician Checks: Dismantles front oil suction plate to inspect motor inlet.

Possible Solution: Chemical wash of filterless oil plate and flush motor drip canal.
