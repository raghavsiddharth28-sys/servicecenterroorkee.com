Local Kitchen Chimney Service in Roorkee

Indian cooking with tadka, spices, and frying produces heavy oil vapor. Over time, these grease particles coat the internal baffle filters, blower fans, and exhaust duct pipes. If left uncleaned, suction drops significantly, smoke fills the kitchen, and oil starts dripping down onto your stovetop.

Our local technicians visit your home to carry out thorough deep cleaning and technical repairs for suction motors, switches, and lighting.

Services We Provide for Kitchen Chimneys

Deep Chimney Cleaning

Dismantling baffle filters, cleaning grease trays, degreasing internal canopy walls, and clearing blower fan blades.

Motor & Noise Repair

Fixing heavy motor humming, abnormal vibration, fan blade alignment, and replacing worn motor capacitors.

Touch, Switch & Light

Repairing unresponsive touch control panels, push button switches, and replacing built-in LED spotlights.

Common Kitchen Chimney Issues Handled

Low Smoke Suction: Clogged mesh or baffle filters blocking the natural airflow into the blower.
Oil Dripping on Cooktop: Full oil collection cup or heavy grease accumulation inside the hood housing.
Loud Motor Sound: Unbalanced blower fan or dry motor bearings needing lubrication or alignment.
Chimney Not Starting: Power socket failure, internal fuse blow, or burnt main motor winding.

Kitchen Chimney Service FAQs

For typical Indian cooking with regular oil and frying, chimney filters should be cleaned every 2 to 3 months, with a deep chemical cleaning once or twice a year to keep the motor running smoothly.
A humming motor without suction usually points to a weak motor capacitor, heavy sticky grease binding the blower wheel, or a jammed motor shaft. A technician visit is recommended to inspect the capacitor and motor safely.
