Common Kutchina Kitchen Chimney Problems & Solutions

Detailed breakdown of genuine issues faced by Kutchina chimney owners in Roorkee kitchens with root causes and repair methods.

1 Kutchina i-Clean Function Not Activating

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina i-clean function not activating.

Possible Reason: Internal cooking hour counter glitch or heating element circuit open.

What Technician Checks: Tests i-Clean relay on motherboard and verifies heating coil continuity.

Possible Solution: Reset i-Clean processor memory or replace thermal auto-clean heating strip.

2 Kutchina Chimney Suction Power Dropping

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina chimney suction power dropping.

Possible Reason: Centrifugal blower impeller packed with thick solidified mustard oil.

What Technician Checks: Measures airflow velocity and inspects blower blade coating depth.

Possible Solution: ₹850 professional chemical deep cleaning to dissolve internal grease.

3 Heavy Motor Vibration Shaking Kitchen Cabinet

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows heavy motor vibration shaking kitchen cabinet.

Possible Reason: Imbalanced blower wheel due to uneven grease or worn motor rubber mount.

What Technician Checks: Inspects blower fan wheel rotation and checks motor mounting bushes.

Possible Solution: Chemical wash of blower wheel and replace vibration dampening rubber bushes.

4 Kutchina Touch Panel Buttons Unresponsive

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina touch panel buttons unresponsive.

Possible Reason: Oil vapor ingress behind glass faceplate shorting capacitive touch traces.

What Technician Checks: Dismantles touch glass to inspect ribbon cable and PCB tracks.

Possible Solution: Clean touch contacts, dry moisture, or replace front touch control board.

5 Kutchina Motor Humming But Not Rotating

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina motor humming but not rotating.

Possible Reason: Run capacitor (4µF-6µF) failed or motor shaft seized by cold grease.

What Technician Checks: Tests capacitor value with digital capacitance meter.

Possible Solution: Replace motor run capacitor and lubricate motor shaft bearings.

6 Oil Dripping from Rear Canal onto Stovetop

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows oil dripping from rear canal onto stovetop.

Possible Reason: Rear oil guide canal choked with hardened sludge; oil cup overflowing.

What Technician Checks: Inspects slope angle of internal oil drainage canal.

Possible Solution: Flush oil canal with chemical solvent and replace cracked oil cup.

7 Smoke Lingering in Kitchen During Deep Frying

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows smoke lingering in kitchen during deep frying.

Possible Reason: Duct pipe has sharp bends or exterior wall louver flap is stuck shut.

What Technician Checks: Checks duct path from chimney collar to outdoor exhaust wall exit.

Possible Solution: Straighten flexible aluminium duct pipe and free outdoor louver flaps.

8 Kutchina Chimney LED Spotlights Not Working

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina chimney led spotlights not working.

Possible Reason: Burnt 12V constant-current LED driver module or blown LED chip.

What Technician Checks: Measures 12V DC output from the internal LED transformer driver.

Possible Solution: Replace 12V LED driver module and fit new warm/white LED spotlight.

9 Kutchina Chimney Suddenly Shuts Off Mid-Cooking

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina chimney suddenly shuts off mid-cooking.

Possible Reason: Motor thermal protection switch (TOP) cutting off due to excessive heat.

What Technician Checks: Measures motor temperature rise during high speed operation.

Possible Solution: Clean choked blower housing to reduce motor load and operating heat.

10 Burning Wire Smell from Kutchina Canopy

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows burning wire smell from kutchina canopy.

Possible Reason: Loose terminal screw causing high contact resistance and melting wire sleeve.

What Technician Checks: Inspects internal electrical terminal box for charred plastic.

Possible Solution: Trim burnt wire, install ceramic connectors, and tighten terminals.

11 Motion Gesture Wave Sensor Not Detecting Hand

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows motion gesture wave sensor not detecting hand.

Possible Reason: Infrared optical sensor window covered in sticky grease mist.

What Technician Checks: Cleans optical sensor lens and checks sensor signal output.

Possible Solution: Clean optical sensor lens or replace gesture sensor board.

12 Kitchen MCB Tripping When Kutchina Starts

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kitchen mcb tripping when kutchina starts.

Possible Reason: Direct electrical short to chassis or motor copper winding grounded.

What Technician Checks: Performs megger insulation test between power leads and metal body.

Possible Solution: Eliminate internal short, insulate wiring joints, or replace motor.

13 Blower Impeller Cracked from Heat Stress

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows blower impeller cracked from heat stress.

Possible Reason: Prolonged high cooking heat causing plastic impeller hub embrittlement.

What Technician Checks: Inspects blower wheel hub for hairline cracks and missing blades.

Possible Solution: Install brand-compatible dynamic balanced blower wheel.

14 Kutchina Push Buttons Stuck in Pressed Position

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina push buttons stuck in pressed position.

Possible Reason: Hardened cooking grease gummed up inside mechanical switch spring.

What Technician Checks: Opens switch bezel and tests spring release mechanism.

Possible Solution: Degrease switch bank with contact cleaner or replace assembly.

15 Flexible Aluminium Duct Crushed in Kitchen Loft

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows flexible aluminium duct crushed in kitchen loft.

Possible Reason: Weight of storage boxes in loft crushed thin foil duct pipe.

What Technician Checks: Inspects duct pipe cross-section area throughout the exhaust path.

Possible Solution: Replace with heavy-gauge flexible aluminium duct pipe.

16 Chimney Body Tilting Forward on Wall

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows chimney body tilting forward on wall.

Possible Reason: Wall anchor rawl plugs loosened from brickwork due to vibration.

What Technician Checks: Checks mounting bracket stability and verifies spirit level.

Possible Solution: Re-fasten with heavy-duty metal anchor expansion bolts.

17 Oil Leaking from Motor Bottom Casing

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows oil leaking from motor bottom casing.

Possible Reason: Internal canopy grease drainage slopes blocked with solid carbon sludge.

What Technician Checks: Opens motor shroud to inspect internal oil drainage channels.

Possible Solution: Complete ₹850 deep chemical degreasing and clean drainage slopes.

18 Motor Runs at Only One Low Speed

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows motor runs at only one low speed.

Possible Reason: Speed tap relay on control board open circuit.

What Technician Checks: Tests voltage delivery across speed 1, 2, and 3 pins on PCB.

Possible Solution: Repair PCB speed control circuit or replace control board.

19 Outdoor Duct Flap Rattling Loudly in Wind

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows outdoor duct flap rattling loudly in wind.

Possible Reason: Non-return valve spring broken or flap loose at duct exit.

What Technician Checks: Inspects duct collar non-return valve flaps.

Possible Solution: Install spring-loaded anti-draft damper valve on exhaust collar.

20 Kutchina Curved Glass Getting Extremely Hot

Problem / Symptom: Kutchina chimney shows kutchina curved glass getting extremely hot.

Possible Reason: Chimney mounted too low or blower not pulling heat away efficiently.

What Technician Checks: Measures height between stovetop and chimney base (recommended 26-30 in).

Possible Solution: Adjust mounting height and restore full motor exhaust suction.
