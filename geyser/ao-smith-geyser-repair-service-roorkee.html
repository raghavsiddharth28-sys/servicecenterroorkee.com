Common AO Smith Geyser Problems & Diagnostic Solutions

Below are the most frequent issues Roorkee homeowners report with AO Smith water heaters, along with our structured diagnostic procedure:

1 AO Smith Geyser Not Heating Water

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith geyser not heating water.

Possible Reason: Burned out Blue Diamond glass-coated heating element or tripped manual safety thermal cutout switch.

What is Checked: Technician tests voltage at the terminal block, verifies thermal cutout switch continuity, and measures coil resistance with a multimeter.

Possible Solution: Replacing the burned heating element with an exact-fit AO Smith compatible coil and resetting safety switch.

2 Water Heating Very Slowly in AO Smith HSE-SDS

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water heating very slowly in ao smith hse-sds.

Possible Reason: Thick calcified mineral sediment encrusting the glass-coated element and accumulating at the tank bottom.

What is Checked: Technician drains the unit, pulls out the element flange, and measures calcium deposit thickness.

Possible Solution: Professional tank descaling, chemical cleaning of the heating element, and sediment flushing.

3 AO Smith Geyser Tripping Bathroom MCB or ELCB

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith geyser tripping bathroom mcb or elcb.

Possible Reason: Internal hairline fracture in the glass-coated element sheath allowing 230V live current to leak into water.

What is Checked: Technician performs an insulation resistance megger test between heating terminals and ground.

Possible Solution: Immediate replacement of the grounded heating element and verifying the bathroom socket earthing connection.

4 AO Smith HeatBot Digital Display Showing Error Code E1 / E2

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith heatbot digital display showing error code e1 / e2.

Possible Reason: Temperature sensor probe (NTC thermistor) out of range or broken sensor wiring connecting to main PCB.

What is Checked: Technician measures sensor resistance at ambient temperature and checks control board connector.

Possible Solution: Replacing the calibrated temperature sensor probe or repairing the digital display controller board.

5 Water Dripping from AO Smith Flange Mounting Base

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water dripping from ao smith flange mounting base.

Possible Reason: Worn or hardened circular rubber flange gasket seal, or unevenly torqued flange mounting bolts.

What is Checked: Technician removes lower plastic cowl to inspect the element flange plate for leaks.

Possible Solution: Installing a new heavy-duty silicone/EPDM flange gasket and torquing bolts evenly to manufacturer spec.

6 Continuous Dripping from AO Smith Multi-Function Safety Valve

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences continuous dripping from ao smith multi-function safety valve.

Possible Reason: Inlet line water pressure exceeding 8 bar or hard water mineral grit trapped under the valve seat.

What is Checked: Technician measures inlet water pressure with a gauge and actuates the manual test lever.

Possible Solution: Flushing mineral grit from valve seat or replacing the 8-bar multi-function safety valve (MFSV).

7 Water Overheating and Scalding Steam Hissing Out

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water overheating and scalding steam hissing out.

Possible Reason: Primary electronic thermostat or capillary stem contacts welded closed in the active heating position.

What is Checked: Technician monitors water temperature during heating to verify if the thermostat disconnects at 75°C.

Possible Solution: Immediate replacement of the faulty primary thermostat and testing secondary high-limit cutout.

8 AO Smith Mini Bot Water Leakage at Pipe Nipples

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith mini bot water leakage at pipe nipples.

Possible Reason: Deteriorated rubber washers inside braided connection hoses or cracked brass nipple threads.

What is Checked: Technician inspects inlet and outlet pipe threads and tests connection washers under pressure.

Possible Solution: Installing heavy-duty EPDM rubber washers and re-sealing threads with high-density PTFE tape.

9 AO Smith Geyser Making Boiling or Knocking Noise

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith geyser making boiling or knocking noise.

Possible Reason: Thermal cavitation caused by steam bubbles trapped under a thick crust of mineral sediment on the element.

What is Checked: Technician listens to acoustic noise during heating cycle and inspects element scale deposit.

Possible Solution: Chemical descaling of the heating element and thoroughly flushing sediment sludge from the tank bottom.

10 Red & Green Power Indicators Not Glowing

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences red & green power indicators not glowing.

Possible Reason: Burnt indicator neon lamps, disconnected spade lugs, or loose wire inside the front panel.

What is Checked: Technician tests voltage supply across indicator terminal connectors.

Possible Solution: Replacing the twin neon indicator lamp assembly and securing wire harness joints.

11 Rusty or Discolored Water from AO Smith Geyser

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences rusty or discolored water from ao smith geyser.

Possible Reason: Depleted sacrificial anode rod allowing mineral attack on exposed inner fittings.

What is Checked: Technician inspects the sacrificial anode condition upon tank drainage.

Possible Solution: Flushing rusty sediment from the tank and installing a fresh heavy-duty anode rod.

12 Low Water Flow from Hot Water Outlet Tap

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences low water flow from hot water outlet tap.

Possible Reason: Inlet non-return safety valve or outlet nipple partially choked with calcium flakes.

What is Checked: Technician compares inlet line pressure with outlet pipe discharge volume.

Possible Solution: Flushing pipe sediment, clearing inlet/outlet nipples, and descaling the non-return valve.

13 Burning Smell Near AO Smith Power Plug

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences burning smell near ao smith power plug.

Possible Reason: High electrical resistance caused by loose terminal screws, oxidized plug pins, or undersized wiring.

What is Checked: Technician checks the 16A plug top, wall socket, and terminal block for heat discoloration.

Possible Solution: Replacing damaged plug top, renewing burnt wire leads, and securing tight terminal connections.

14 AO Smith Elegance Temperature Knob Spinning Freely

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith elegance temperature knob spinning freely.

Possible Reason: Broken plastic control shaft connecting the external dial to the internal thermostat spindle.

What is Checked: Technician removes the front dial cover to inspect the mechanical coupling.

Possible Solution: Replacing the knob coupling or installing a new adjustable capillary thermostat.

15 AO Smith Geyser Shuts OFF in 2 Minutes

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith geyser shuts off in 2 minutes.

Possible Reason: Thermostat pocket inside the tank is heavily coated with lime scale, causing false high-temperature readings.

What is Checked: Technician inspects the thermostat pocket tube for mineral insulation that traps heat near the sensor.

Possible Solution: Descaling the sensor pocket tube and recalibrating or replacing the stem thermostat.

16 Unusual Electrical Humming Sound from Base

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences unusual electrical humming sound from base.

Possible Reason: Loose heating element bracket vibrating under 50Hz AC current or loose wall anchor bolts.

What is Checked: Technician inspects element mounting tightness, wall bracket stability, and water inlet pressure.

Possible Solution: Tightening element bracket screws, securing wall anchors, and verifying steady water flow.

17 AO Smith Instant Geyser Flow Sensor Failure

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences ao smith instant geyser flow sensor failure.

Possible Reason: Magnetic reed switch flow sensor jammed with sand or mineral scale, preventing heating activation.

What is Checked: Technician tests magnetic flow switch continuity while running cold water through the unit.

Possible Solution: Cleaning the flow sensor chamber or replacing the electronic flow switch module.

18 Inner Blue Diamond Tank Leakage on Older Geyser

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences inner blue diamond tank leakage on older geyser.

Possible Reason: Extreme hard water pitting corrosion after many years without sacrificial anode replacement.

What is Checked: Technician strips the outer jacket to verify whether the inner pressure vessel is compromised.

Possible Solution: Honest advice: When an inner tank has rusted through, unit replacement is advised because welding under pressure is hazardous.

Crucial Electrical & Gas Geyser Safety Information

Water heaters operate with high electrical wattage (2000W to 4500W) or pressurized flammable LPG gas directly in wet bathroom environments. Unqualified DIY tinkering poses severe shock and fire hazards.

  • Never attempt DIY electrical repairs: Do not open lower plastic enclosures with power connected. Live 230V terminals can cause lethal electric shock.
  • Gas geyser safety: Never attempt DIY repairs on gas valves, burner manifolds, or combustion chambers. If you detect an LPG gas smell or see yellow soot, turn off the cylinder valve immediately, ventilate the bathroom, and call for professional inspection.
  • Always maintain proper earthing: Ensure your bathroom 16A socket has verified earthing continuity to protect against earth leakage.
