Common Havells Geyser Problems & Diagnostic Solutions

Below are the most frequent issues Roorkee homeowners report with Havells water heaters, along with our structured diagnostic procedure:

1 Havells Geyser Not Heating Water

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells geyser not heating water.

Possible Reason: Burned out Incoloy 800 heating element coil or disconnected power lead at the internal terminal block.

What is Checked: Technician uses a digital multimeter to measure resistance across the element terminals (normal 2kW reads 25-28 ohms) and checks line voltage.

Possible Solution: Replacement of the burned heating element with an original-specification Incoloy or copper element and terminal tightening.

2 Water Heating Very Slowly in Havells Monza

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water heating very slowly in havells monza.

Possible Reason: Heavy calcium scale blanket coating the heating element, insulating heat from transferring into surrounding water.

What is Checked: Technician drains the tank and pulls out the flange assembly to inspect the thickness of hard water scale encrustation.

Possible Solution: Descaling of the heating element and inner tank flushing, or coil replacement if scaling has eroded the metal sheath.

3 Havells Instanio LED Ring Light Stays Blue (No Heating)

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells instanio led ring light stays blue (no heating).

Possible Reason: Secondary thermal cutout switch has tripped due to low water flow while the high-wattage element was powered.

What is Checked: Technician checks water pressure through the unit, tests thermal cutout continuity, and inspects the manual reset pin.

Possible Solution: Resetting the thermal cutout, verifying minimum water flow rate, and testing heating element continuity.

4 Havells Adonia Digital Display or Touch Panel Unresponsive

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells adonia digital display or touch panel unresponsive.

Possible Reason: Internal low-voltage power supply failure or moisture buildup behind the front electronic display panel.

What is Checked: Technician inspects internal PCB ribbon cable, checks DC voltage outputs from the transformer, and tests for moisture corrosion.

Possible Solution: Cleaning and drying PCB traces, re-seating cable harnesses, or replacing the digital control board.

5 Havells Geyser Tripping Bathroom MCB or ELCB

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells geyser tripping bathroom mcb or elcb.

Possible Reason: Micro-cracks in the heating element sheath allowing electrical current to leak into the water, causing an earth leakage fault.

What is Checked: Technician performs an insulation resistance test (megger test) between heating terminals and the earth ground.

Possible Solution: Immediate replacement of the grounded heating element and inspection of the home's 16A earthing connection.

6 Water Dripping from Havells Bottom Plastic Cover

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water dripping from havells bottom plastic cover.

Possible Reason: Hard water corrosion or heat degradation of the triangular flange gasket seal.

What is Checked: Technician removes the bottom cover to isolate whether water leaks from the flange seal bolts or inner tank weld.

Possible Solution: Installation of a new heavy-duty heat-resistant flange gasket and torque-tightening the mounting bolts evenly.

7 Continuous Dripping from Multi-Function Safety Valve (MFSV)

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences continuous dripping from multi-function safety valve (mfsv).

Possible Reason: Excess water expansion pressure in high-rise buildings or lime scale stuck on the valve rubber diaphragm.

What is Checked: Technician tests line water pressure with a gauge and actuates the manual test lever on the safety valve.

Possible Solution: Flushing mineral grit from the valve seat or replacing the worn safety valve. Recommending a pressure reducing valve if inlet pressure exceeds 8 bar.

8 Water Overheating and Scalding Steam Coming Out

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences water overheating and scalding steam coming out.

Possible Reason: Primary stem thermostat contacts fused closed, causing the element to run continuously without shutoff.

What is Checked: Technician monitors water temperature during heating and verifies if the thermostat clicks open at the calibrated 65°C-75°C threshold.

Possible Solution: Immediate replacement of the faulty primary stem thermostat and testing of the secondary safety cutout.

9 Havells Geyser Making Humming or Vibrating Noise

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells geyser making humming or vibrating noise.

Possible Reason: Loose heating coil bracket vibrating under AC electromagnetic frequency or loose wall anchor bolts.

What is Checked: Technician inspects element mounting tightness, wall bracket stability, and checks water inlet pressure.

Possible Solution: Tightening element bracket screws, securing wall anchors with heavy-duty metal fasteners, and adjusting water flow.

10 Havells Instanio Red / Blue Light Not Changing

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells instanio red / blue light not changing.

Possible Reason: Faulty bi-color LED indicator module or broken sensor wire running from the thermostat to the indicator board.

What is Checked: Technician tests voltage at the indicator input leads when the thermostat cycles between heating and cutoff modes.

Possible Solution: Replacement of the LED indicator assembly and securing wiring connections.

11 Rusty or Brown Water Coming from Havells Geyser

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences rusty or brown water coming from havells geyser.

Possible Reason: The sacrificial magnesium anode rod has dissolved completely, allowing mineral attack on the tank fittings.

What is Checked: Technician drains the geyser and removes the anode rod for visual inspection.

Possible Solution: Flushing tank sediment and installing a new heavy magnesium anode rod to restore galvanic protection.

12 Low Hot Water Flow from Havells Geyser Tap

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences low hot water flow from havells geyser tap.

Possible Reason: Lime scale choking the cold water inlet diffuser or clogged non-return safety valve screen.

What is Checked: Technician measures water flow at the inlet hose versus the outlet tap to pinpoint flow constriction.

Possible Solution: Cleaning the inlet mesh filter, descaling the safety valve, and clearing pipe nipple debris.

13 Burning Smell Near Havells Geyser Wall Plug

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences burning smell near havells geyser wall plug.

Possible Reason: Loose contact in the 16A wall socket or melted wire lugs due to high electrical contact resistance.

What is Checked: Technician inspects power plug pins, wall socket contacts, and internal terminal block for scorch marks.

Possible Solution: Replacing damaged plug tops, trimming burnt wires, and installing heat-resistant ceramic terminal connectors.

14 Havells Geyser Shuts OFF in Just 2 Minutes

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells geyser shuts off in just 2 minutes.

Possible Reason: Thermostat pocket inside the tank is heavily coated with lime scale, causing false high-temperature readings.

What is Checked: Technician inspects the thermostat pocket tube for mineral insulation that traps heat near the sensor.

Possible Solution: Descaling the sensor pocket tube and recalibrating or replacing the stem thermostat.

15 Havells Puro Water Temperature Knob Rotating Freely

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells puro water temperature knob rotating freely.

Possible Reason: Cracked plastic spindle connecting the external front knob to the internal thermostat shaft.

What is Checked: Technician disassembles the front casing to check the mechanical knob coupling.

Possible Solution: Replacing the rotary adjustment knob spindle or replacing the capillary adjustable thermostat.

16 Havells Instant Geyser Leaking from Pipe Joints

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences havells instant geyser leaking from pipe joints.

Possible Reason: Hardened or split rubber washers inside the flexible stainless steel braided connection hoses.

What is Checked: Technician inspects inlet and outlet pipe threads and tests connection washers under pressure.

Possible Solution: Installing fresh heavy-duty EPDM rubber washers and re-sealing threads with high-density PTFE tape.

17 Popping Kettle Noise While Havells Geyser Heats

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences popping kettle noise while havells geyser heats.

Possible Reason: Thermal cavitation caused by steam bubbles trapped under a thick crust of mineral sediment on the element.

What is Checked: Technician listens to acoustic noise during heating cycle and inspects element scale deposit.

Possible Solution: Chemical descaling of the heating element and thoroughly flushing sediment sludge from the tank bottom.

18 Inner Feroglas Tank Leakage on Older Havells Geyser

Problem / Notice: Customer experiences inner feroglas tank leakage on older havells geyser.

Possible Reason: Severe pitting corrosion along the tank seam after many years of high-mineral hard water exposure.

What is Checked: Technician removes outer PUF insulation casing to inspect the steel tank integrity.

Possible Solution: Honest evaluation: If the inner tank has corroded through, tank replacement or unit replacement is advised as patch welding is unsafe on pressurized tanks.

Crucial Electrical & Gas Geyser Safety Information

Water heaters operate with high electrical wattage (2000W to 4500W) or pressurized flammable LPG gas directly in wet bathroom environments. Unqualified DIY tinkering poses severe shock and fire hazards.

  • Never attempt DIY electrical repairs: Do not open lower plastic enclosures with power connected. Live 230V terminals can cause lethal electric shock.
  • Gas geyser safety: Never attempt DIY repairs on gas valves, burner manifolds, or combustion chambers. If you detect an LPG gas smell or see yellow soot, turn off the cylinder valve immediately, ventilate the bathroom, and call for professional inspection.
  • Always maintain proper earthing: Ensure your bathroom 16A socket has verified earthing continuity to protect against earth leakage.
