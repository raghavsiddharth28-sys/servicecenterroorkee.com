Common Jaquar Water Geyser Faults Diagnosed & Fixed Locally

⚠ Water Not Heating at All / Power LED Remains Off

Typical Cause: Failed immersion heating element, blown thermal cut-out, or tripped reset button.

Doorstep Solution: Immersion heating element replacement and high-limit thermostat reset.

⚠ Water Overheating / Steam Coming from Hot Water Tap

Typical Cause: Thermostat contact welded together or capillary tube sensor malfunction.

Doorstep Solution: Stem or capillary thermostat replacement and temperature calibration.

⚠ Water Leaking from Bottom Cover or Safety Valve

Typical Cause: Pressure relief valve (PRV) dripping, tank gasket failure, or inner tank weld pinhole.

Doorstep Solution: PRV valve replacement, silicone tank gasket sealing, or inlet union repair.

⚠ Home MCB Tripping Instantly When Geyser Switched On

Typical Cause: Internal heating element casing cracked allowing water to contact live wire.

Doorstep Solution: Immediate element replacement and complete electrical insulation resistance test.

⚠ Very Low Hot Water Flow Compared to Cold Tap

Typical Cause: Heavy calcium scale buildup in outlet pipe nipple or mineral sludge in tank bottom.

Doorstep Solution: Complete tank descaling, pipe clearing, and sacrificial magnesium anode rod renewal.
