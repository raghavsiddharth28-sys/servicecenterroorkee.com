Common Prestige Kitchen Chimney Faults Diagnosed & Fixed Locally

⚠ Severe Loss of Suction / Smoke Filling the Kitchen

Typical Cause: Oil-saturated baffle filters, choked duct mesh, or weak blower capacitor.

Doorstep Solution: Deep degreasing, duct pipe inspection, and blower motor capacitor upgrade.

⚠ Excessive Motor Humming & Rattling Vibration

Typical Cause: Blower fan wheel grease unbalance, dry motor bearings, or loose mounting bracket.

Doorstep Solution: Impeller cleaning, bearing lubrication, and secure wall-mount re-tightening.

⚠ Auto-Clean Heating Element Not Melting Grease

Typical Cause: Failed heating strip coil or faulty auto-clean relay on the control PCB.

Doorstep Solution: Heating element continuity test, thermal fuse replacement, or PCB relay repair.

⚠ Touch Sensor / Motion Gesture Control Unresponsive

Typical Cause: Grease coating optical sensors, moisture on touch PCB, or power fluctuation.

Doorstep Solution: Sensor window cleaning, touch panel recalibration, or display PCB service.

⚠ Oil Dripping from Chimney Edges or Ceiling Duct

Typical Cause: Full oil collector cup, cracked oil channel tray, or loose duct tape seal.

Doorstep Solution: Oil collector emptying, channel realigning, and heat-resistant aluminum duct sealing.
